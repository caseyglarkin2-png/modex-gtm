## Step 0 — Location confirmation
Roster geocode (42.276984, -83.413602, ROOFTOP, moved 170 m) landed on the
plant rooftop. Satellite probes at z15-z17 confirm the unmistakable large
assembly complex on the south side of Michigan Ave. Web research (Ford
corporate, Ford Authority, Wikipedia) confirms ~5M sq ft of building on a
369-acre site. The site contains two large building clusters separated by an
active rail corridor.

## Key views
- **z15 wide:** Two large building clusters — the Michigan Assembly Plant
  along Michigan Ave to the north, and a connected integrated stamping building

  trailer rows fill the central/south yard.
- **South side (z18/z19):** Rail yard with auto-rack rail cars loaded with
  finished vehicles; rows of parked trailers (drop yards) on both sides of the
  rail.

  controlling internal access.
- **SW (z19):** Finished-vehicle staging lots with rail loading platforms.
- **Street View:** Michigan Ave frontage is buffered by woods and residential
