# Ford - Parts Redistribution Center, Brownstown Township MI — Deep Audit

## Resolved location
- Roster coords (42.176414, -83.281372) land squarely on a massive distribution
  warehouse — they are correct (the geocode "movedMeters 5444" was measured against an
  earlier wrong point, not a current error).
- Web research confirms the facility: **Ford Parts Redistribution Center, 25555
  Pennsylvania Rd, Brownstown Charter Twp / Romulus, MI 48174** — a Ford Customer
  Service Division redistribution center.
- **Locked center: 42.17640, -83.28120.**

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **truckGate = true.** Continuous chain-link perimeter fence rings the property
  (confirmed in 2024 Street View along Inkster Rd). The site is entered via a single
  long (~600 m) private access road from Pennsylvania Rd to the north — a controlled,
  set-back entrance.
- **guardShack = false (low confidence).** The access road is private and Street View
  does not travel down it. A small structure is visible near the building-end yard
  entrance but cannot be confirmed as a staffed guard booth. Flagged uncertain.
- **remoteGs = true (low confidence).** Controlled gate, no confirmed booth.
- **dockDoors = "50+".** Dock doors with trailers backed in along both the north and
  south building faces; ~90 estimated.
- **dropArea = "50+".** Very large organized trailer-storage yards north and south —
  over 200 trailers visible.
- **dropYard = true.** Two dedicated trailer-storage lots packed with trailers.
- **shipRcvSeparate = true.** Dock banks on physically separate north and south faces.
- **railServed = false.** A rail line passes well east of the site; no spur enters.
- **preGateStaging = true.** The long private access road provides ample off-road
  truck waiting space before the secured yard.
