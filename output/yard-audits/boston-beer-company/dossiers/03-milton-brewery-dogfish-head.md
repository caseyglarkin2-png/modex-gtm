## Location confirmation
The roster's ROOFTOP geocode (38.770294, -75.310106) landed directly on the
brewery building. Satellite probing (z17-z20) confirmed a large production
brewery: extensive outdoor fermentation/storage tank farms, brewing process
equipment, a dedicated on-site wastewater treatment plant (large circular
clarifier) on the NE, and a green-painted multi-bay industrial building. Web
research and Dogfish Head's own site confirm 6 Cannery Village Center as the
Milton main production brewery (acquired by Boston Beer in 2019). Street View
also surfaced interior brewery imagery (400-BBL fermentation tanks) and
Dogfish Head's signature whimsical structures, all consistent with this site.
