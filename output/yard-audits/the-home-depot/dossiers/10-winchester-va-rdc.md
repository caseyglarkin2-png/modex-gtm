park on the west side of Winchester, VA, immediately adjacent to Interstate 81.

as a ~466,000 sq ft Rapid Deployment Center, 24/5 operations. Satellite imagery
shows loading docks on both long faces with trailers backed in and large

despite the geocode offset flag.
