## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate (true, medium):** The plant runs a formal numbered-gate security system
  (the "Gate 12" sign), with a continuous chain-link perimeter fence. The gate barriers
  sit at the ends of private access drives, beyond Street View coverage, so the barrier
  arm could not be individually imaged — but a controlled truck entrance is well
  established.
- **Guard shack (true, medium):** A flagship secured assembly plant with numbered gates
  almost always staffs its gates. A guard booth could not be directly imaged because SV
  does not reach the gate itself; classified true with the flag listed uncertain.
- **Docks:** ~40 dock doors across multiple building faces — band 25-50. Receiving (NW,
  raw material by truck and rail) and the SE finished-goods shipping yard appear
  physically separate.
- **Driveway:** Long private access drives hold a 3+ truck queue.
- **Rail:** TRUE — multiple rail tracks run through and alongside the plant.
