## Location confirmation
Roster supplied geometric-center coordinates (42.522441, -92.269194) flagged as moved
2958 m. Satellite probe at z15-z16 landed directly on a massive industrial complex
surrounded entirely by farmland — unmistakably the John Deere Waterloo Works Tractor
Operations site (the largest Deere assembly complex in the world, building 7/8/9 Series
tractors). Web search confirmed 3500 E Donald St, Waterloo IA, with public 90-minute
riding factory tours. Center refined slightly SW to 42.5210, -92.2695 to sit on the
building mass.

## What the key views showed
- **z15/z16 context:** Diagonal NW-SE building complex roughly 1.5 km x 1.3 km, ringed by
  cropland; two large retention ponds on the south side; large employee parking lot on
  the NW corner.
- **z17/z18 mid views:** Extensive trailer parking and finished-goods staging. The central
  yard between building wings holds 60-90+ trailers in organized rows (drop yard).
- **z18-z20 dock views:** Long building wings with regular dock-door rhythm and trailers
  backed in along the east and south faces; estimated ~45 dock doors total.
- **SE corner (z18, 42.5125,-92.260):** A separate cluster of staging buildings (brown- and
  blue-roof structures) with extensive rows of finished tractors staged for shipment.
- **Internal test loop (z19, 42.517,-92.266):** An on-site tractor proving/test loop with
  small ancillary buildings — consistent with an ag-tractor assembly plant.
- **Street View:** Coverage exists only on the surrounding public rural roads (E Donald St,
  41st St, the SE road junction at 42.5142,-92.2585 with a wide concrete truck-turn apron).
  No Street View pano reaches the actual plant truck gate — the complex sits well back
  behind long private internal drives.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate (true, medium):** Could not be directly imaged because Street View stops at
  public roads. However, a Fortune-500 flagship assembly campus of this scale, set back
  behind >1 km of private internal drive and running public tours, is universally fenced
  and gate-controlled. The SE staging-area driveway shows a small structure/sign at the
  road. Classified true with the flag listed as uncertain.
- **Guard shack (true, medium):** Standard staffed security post inferred for a plant of
  this caliber; not directly confirmed in imagery.
- **Docks:** ~45 dock doors across multiple building faces — band 25-50. Ship/receive
  appear to run from physically separate building faces (separate clusters).
- **Driveway:** Internal approach drives from the SE road junction to the docks are very
  long — easily a 3+ truck queue (drivewayLong).

## Web findings
Silos & Smokestacks and Iowa DNR Facility Explorer confirm the Tractor Operations facility
at 3500 E Donald St. Site dates to 1918; flagship of Deere's global manufacturing network;
public riding tours run year-round Monday-Friday.

## Final confidence
**medium** — location and overall layout are unambiguous and well-imaged, but the truck
gate and guard shack could not be directly confirmed because Street View does not reach
the internal plant gate. Gate flags, lane counts, scale, ship/receive separation and
multi-step are flagged uncertain.
