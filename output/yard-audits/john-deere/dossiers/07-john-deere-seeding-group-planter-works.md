# Deep-Audit Dossier — John Deere Seeding Group / Planter Works, Moline IL (idx 7)

- **Locked center:** 41.50849, -90.531986 (on the main building roof)
- **Confirmation:** Roster coords were ROOFTOP-precise (moved 129 m). Satellite
