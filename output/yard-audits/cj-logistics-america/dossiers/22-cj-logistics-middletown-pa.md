## Location resolution
The roster supplied no street address (source: Indeed CJ Logistics America
locations list) and city-level coords (40.1996, -76.7313) that land in
downtown Middletown borough — a residential area with no industrial
buildings.

Web research confirmed CJ Logistics operates a Middletown PA 17057
facility. The only large industrial cluster in that ZIP is the **Capital
Logistics Center (CLC)** — a ~1.3M sq ft, 5-building multi-tenant logistics
park off Eisenhower Boulevard / Capital Lane / Industrial Lane in Lower
Swatara Township, immediately adjacent to the Penn State Harrisburg campus.
Building 500 (500 Industrial Lane) and Building 200 (200 Capital Lane) are
documented CLC addresses; CBRE/Cushman & Wakefield list space there.

Public records do **not** identify which CLC building CJ Logistics leases.
I audited **Building C** — the largest single building in the park
(~600 ft long) — as the best-fit representative CJ Logistics DC. This
building-level identification is UNCERTAIN and is the reason confidence
is "low".
