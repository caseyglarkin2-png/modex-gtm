## Location confirmation — UNRESOLVED
The roster supplied no street address and a GEOMETRIC_CENTER geocode of
"CenterPoint Intermodal Center, Elwood." That point (41.391443, -88.153535)
lands squarely inside the BNSF Logistics Park Chicago intermodal rail yard
(rows of containers and rail tracks) — not a building.

Web research established the facility's real address: CJ Logistics' own
open-space sales sheet lists **Elwood 2 at 21012 W. Mississippi Street, Elwood,
IL**, Q4 2025, 1.1M SF, "SPACE IS OPEN." This is the NEXUS Project second
Elwood distribution center — a build-to-suit by ARCO/Murray, groundbreaking
10 October 2024, full operations expected H1 2026.

Despite extensive satellite probing along the W Mississippi Street corridor
and across the CenterPoint Intermodal Center (east, west, south of the existing
CJ LC1 at 21705 W Mississippi St), **no distinct construction site or new
building could be positively matched to 21012 W Mississippi St.** Current Maxar
imagery shows the corridor fully built out with dozens of near-identical large
warehouses; the new CJ building cannot be told apart from its neighbors, and no
published coordinates or parcel-precise location exist. The exact building
remains unresolved.
