# Deep-Audit Dossier — CJ Logistics, Mira Loma CA (idx 15)

## Facility
- **Name:** CJ Logistics - Mira Loma CA
- **Type:** Distribution Center
- **Address:** 12350 Philadelphia Avenue, Mira Loma (Eastvale), CA 91752
- **Resolved center:** 34.0319, -117.5520

## Location confirmation
The roster geocode (34.032307, -117.552288, ROOFTOP, moved 5262 m) landed on a
large warehouse complex on Philadelphia Avenue. Web search confirmed 12350
Philadelphia Ave as "DSC Logistics Bldg 3" (Yelp, LoopNet APN 156-020-027,
clustrmaps), associated with CJ Logistics America (DSC was acquired by CJ
Logistics). The site is a large N-S oriented distribution warehouse running
parallel to and directly adjacent to the I-15 freeway. Probed satellite at
z16-z20 and Street View around the perimeter to lock the building.

## Site layout
- Single very large rectangular warehouse, oriented N-S, ~1M sq ft class.
- **North:** Philadelphia Avenue; landscaped NW corner with curving access
  road and employee car parking lot.
- **East:** I-15 freeway; the main truck court / drop yard sits between the
  building's east face and the freeway buffer.
- **South:** A rail mainline and a wide industrial arterial (Cornerstone Dr
  area); chain-link perimeter fence visible along the south.
- **West:** A narrow fenced alley separates this building from an adjacent
  warehouse of similar size (separate property).

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **truckGate: false.** No barrier arm, sliding/swing gate, or checkpoint
  pinch-point visible at any access point. The truck court connects to the
  public road network via an open paved driveway at the SW corner. Typical
  open multi-tenant Inland Empire industrial-park access.
- **guardShack: false.** No staffed booth structure beside any entrance in
  the Street View probes.
- **remoteGs: false.** No gate, therefore no remote check-in.
- **dockDoors: 50+.** Regular bay rhythm along the east face and south face;
  estimated ~70 doors total (low confidence on exact count).
- **dropArea / dropYard: 50+ / true.** Dedicated trailer drop yard on the
  east side, filled with trailers in marked stalls; second drop area at the
  SW corner.

## Web findings
12350 Philadelphia Ave is the former DSC Logistics Bldg 3, now operating
under CJ Logistics America (CJ acquired DSC Logistics in 2018). General
warehousing and storage. A Walmart trailer was visible near the site in
Street View — the surrounding park hosts multiple large 3PL/retail DCs.

## Confidence
**Medium.** Building identity and overall layout are clear. Gate/guard-shack
calls rely on Street View that could not get onto the internal driveway
(rail spur blocks direct road access); no control structures were visible,
supporting an open-access classification. Dock-door count is an overhead
estimate.
