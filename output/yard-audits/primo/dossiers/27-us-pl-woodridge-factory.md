# Site 27 — US PL Woodridge Factory (BlueTriton / Primo)

**Resolved location:** 10335 Argonne Woods Dr, Woodridge, IL 60517 (DuPage County, Chicago metro)
**Audited center:** 41.69905, -88.00875
**Maps (3D):** https://www.google.com/maps/@41.69905,-88.00875,400m/data=!3m1!1e3

## Operational verdict: OPERATIONAL — but it is NOT a bottling plant

This is a **blank-flag** entry on the BlueTriton facility list typed as a
"Bottling plant (PL)." That type is a **mislabel** for this address. The Woodridge
site is BlueTriton/Primo's **ReadyRefresh delivery branch** (Ice Mountain Direct /
home-and-office water delivery), occupying a suite (Suite 200) in a multi-tenant
industrial park. Ice Mountain spring water is bottled in **Stanwood, MI** — not here.

**Operational status confirmed ACTIVE** by 2025 BlueTriton job postings geo-tagged
to Woodridge IL: Route Delivery Drivers (CDL and non-CDL, commission), Heavy
Equipment Mechanic, Order Manager, Back-of-House Shift Manager, and a **Branch
Operations Manager** for this facility. No closure or demolition news exists; the
satellite shows an active operation (delivery box-truck/cube-van fleet staged,
trailers backed to docks, a forklift moving in the court). So: NOT closed, NOT
demolished, NOT repurposed — running, just as a distribution/route branch rather
than a plant.

  the shared court. (Listed uncertain — a tenant could badge a man-door, but no
  vehicular gate exists on imagery.)
- **guardShack: FALSE.** No booth-sized structure at any entrance.
- **remoteGs: FALSE.** No gate exists, so by rubric this is false.

  drop/staging lot distinct from the active dock faces (~10-15 trailers/box trucks

## Confidence: MEDIUM
Location and operational status are firmly established (high confidence on both).
The classification is medium because this is a **multi-tenant** building: the exact
suite footprint and which docks/trailers belong to the BlueTriton route branch
versus other tenants cannot be isolated from overhead imagery. Counts and the
ship/receive split are therefore banded conservatively and flagged uncertain. The
key sales-relevant facts hold regardless: open multi-tenant park, no gate, no guard
shack, modest shared dock/drop footprint, dense urban setting, no rail.
