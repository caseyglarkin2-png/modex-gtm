# Site 32 — US PL Saratoga Spring Factory

**Facility:** Saratoga Spring Water Company / BlueTriton (now Primo Brands) bottling plant
**Address:** 11 Geyser Road (NY Route 29), Saratoga Springs, NY 12866
**Type:** Bottling plant (PL)
**Resolved center:** 43.05895, -73.81095
**Confidence:** high

## Location resolution
The original approximate coordinates (43.0710, -73.7846) landed ~3 km NE on a
cemetery and were discarded. The supplied resolved address point
(43.0587, -73.8105) was used as the anchor. Web search confirmed the Saratoga
Spring Water Company bottling plant at 11 Geyser Road, founded 1872 and the
oldest continuously bottling water company at the same site; acquired by
BlueTriton Brands in late 2021, now part of Primo Brands after the BlueTriton/
Primo Water merger. Satellite at z18/z19 confirmed a compact industrial
bottling complex on the north side of Geyser Road, with the Kayaderosseras
Creek wrapping the east edge — consistent with the brand's spring-water source.
The exact bottling-plant buildings were pinned and the yard center locked at
43.05895, -73.81095.

## What each key view showed
- **z18 wide / z19 overall:** A silver/blue metal warehouse (NW, runs NW–SE),
  an older gray-roofed bottling/process building (center), and a blue process/
  office section fronting Geyser Road (SE). Geyser Road runs E–W along the south
  edge; the creek borders the east. Compact site, not a large DC.
- **z20 center + z21 dock face:** The SE face of the NW warehouse carries a low
  canopy dock line with a single 53-ft trailer backed in, a tractor and pickup
  in the apron, and blue water tanks/silos. Dock count reads ~4–8 → band 0-10.
- **z20 west (trailers):** A diagonal cluster of ~8–9 trailers/storage
  containers parked without tractors in the rear (NW/west) lot, plus an
  employee car row. This is the drop yard.
- **z20 entrance / SE docks:** The buildings sit very close to Route 29; the
  internal paved apron between the warehouse, bottling building, and creek
  serves as the turn-around / staging area.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate: FALSE (definitive).** The single main truck driveway is a
  wide-open paved curb cut directly off Geyser Road. Street View pano
  `DsolxoLUGNLjF2lNdtVaeA` (2022-03), viewed at headings 0°, 25°, and 340°, shows
  no barrier arm, no sliding/swing gate, and no checkpoint pinch-point. A
  green-painted one-way/directional marking exists on the apron but is not a
  physical control.
- **Guard shack: FALSE (definitive).** No booth-sized structure (1–3 vehicle
  footprint, multi-side windows) at the entrance in any Street View heading or
  satellite tile. Nearest structure is the warehouse itself.
- **Remote GS: FALSE.** No truck gate exists, so no kiosk/call-box check-in
  applies.
- **Fencing:** The rear west trailer-storage lot IS chain-link fenced (visible
  in far-west pano `n5QU41fdyM2INP7UXhggTw`), but the active truck entrance is
  open and uncontrolled.
- **Docks: 0-10 band.** Single canopy-covered dock line on the warehouse SE
  face; ~4–8 doors. Low confidence due to the canopy obscuring door rhythm.

## Web findings
Saratoga Spring Water Co. (est. 1872) HQ + bottling plant at 11 Geyser Road;
acquired by BlueTriton Brands (Nov 2021), now Primo Brands. Sparkling/still
water in the cobalt-blue bottle. Listed as "BlueTriton Brands, Inc – Saratoga
Factory" in the Saratoga County Chamber of Commerce. This is the active legacy
plant with current satellite + Street View coverage.

## Final confidence
**High.** Location is unambiguous and well-corroborated. Gate, guard-shack, and
remote-GS calls are confirmed FALSE from clear multi-heading 2022 Street View at
the entrance. Only dockDoors and shipRcvSeparate are flagged uncertain (canopy
obscures door count; ship/receive function not confirmable from imagery).
