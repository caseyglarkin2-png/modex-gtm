## Location resolution

The roster geocode (32.705501, -83.649254, ROOFTOP, moved 61 m) lands inside the
large airport-area industrial complex on Airport Drive, ~1 km from Middle
Georgia Regional Airport. The complex contains two very large industrial
buildings. Street View from the south road (2023-07) shows the **"Pactiv
Evergreen"** blue-logo sign on the south face of the **center/east building** —
that is the plant. The geocode point sits on the western half of this building.
Web search corroborates: Yelp, paper-world.com, and local news (a 2024 fire in
"egg-crate-type product") all place the Pactiv molded-fibre plant at 7670
Airport Drive. The upper-left building of the complex is a separate occupant and
was not audited.
