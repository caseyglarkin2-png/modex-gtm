## Location confirmation — ROSTER COORDINATE CORRECTION
The supplied roster coordinates (and the "Killingly Commons Dr" address) pointed at the
**Killingly Commons retail shopping center** — a big-box plaza with parking lots, not an
industrial plant. Web research established the real plant address as **1886 Upper Maple
St, Dayville, CT**. Probing ~2.3 km NW revealed the actual Frito-Lay Killingly
manufacturing plant: a large industrial building with extensive trailer drop yards,
bordered by a lake/pond to the west and I-395 to the east. Locked center at ~41.8605,
-71.8905.

## Web findings
PotatoPro, the NECT Chamber of Commerce, and Powder & Bulk Solids confirm the Killingly
plant began production in 1980, employs ~700 people, serves as both a production plant
and distribution center, and was the subject of a $235M expansion project. It is
Connecticut's first existing manufacturing site to achieve LEED Gold.
