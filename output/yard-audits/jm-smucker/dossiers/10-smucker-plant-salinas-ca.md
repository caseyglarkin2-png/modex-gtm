## IMPORTANT — closure / repurposing
The J.M. Smucker Salinas plant — built 1960/61 to make apple butter, preserves,
jellies and ice cream toppings for the western U.S. — was **closed by the end of
2005**, with production redistributed to Orrville OH and Memphis TN (~168
employees offered jobs elsewhere). It is **no longer a J.M. Smucker facility**.
The building at 1275 Hansen St is now registered to **Taylor Farms Retail** (a
fresh-produce company) in the EPA FRS facility registry. The yard audit below
describes the current physical site for completeness.

## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster supplied only a city-level address ("Salinas, CA 93901"); the
APPROXIMATE geocode (36.662382, -121.645422) landed in a residential
neighborhood roughly 4 km from any industrial site — clearly not the plant. Web
research resolved the historic Smucker Salinas plant to **1275 Hansen St** via
the Macrae's Blue Book business directory, corroborated by the EPA FRS registry,
which lists 1275 Hansen St (now Taylor Farms Retail). Satellite probes at that
location showed a large multi-building food-processing complex with rooftop
solar, solar parking canopies, process equipment, materials/pallet yards, and
trailer staging — consistent with a former preserves plant. The audit center was
relocated there.
