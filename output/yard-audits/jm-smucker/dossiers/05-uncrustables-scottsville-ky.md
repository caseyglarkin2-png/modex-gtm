## Location confirmation

The roster pin (36.764341, -86.183123, ROOFTOP) landed ~1400 m off the building
centroid, on the NW edge of the plant property. Satellite probing immediately
revealed the plant: a large irregular white food-manufacturing building with
processing silos, two large trailer drop yards (NE and SW) and extensive dock
banks — consistent with the J.M. Smucker Uncrustables Scottsville plant. Web
research confirms this as the original Uncrustables plant (opened 2004, ~180,000
sq ft, since expanded by 80,000+ sq ft; also makes the Snack-N-Waffle line).

Street View (March 2026) confirmed the plant building and a "Receiving /
Employee Entrance" sign at the access driveway off Smucker Way. Locked center:
36.76280, -86.18150.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — FALSE.** The access driveway off Smucker Way (signed "Receiving
  / Employee Entrance") is a wide OPEN driveway with no barrier arm, sliding gate
  or checkpoint pinch-point at the public road (confirmed in March 2026 Street
  View). The truck route runs in openly and loops around the building to the
  dock yards. No controlled gate at the property line.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No guard booth at the entrance, the public-road
  intersection, or anywhere along the frontage in Street View or satellite.
- **Remote check-in — FALSE.** No gate, so the flag does not apply.
- **Docks — "25-50" band (~38 doors est.).** Dock banks with trailers backed in
  on the NE building face and a separate dock cluster on the SW face. Flagged
  low confidence (some faces shadowed).
- **Drop yard — TRUE, "50+" band.** Two large dedicated trailer drop yards (NE
  and SW) holding dozens of trailers in marked stalls; ~65 trailers visible,
  capacity ~100 combined.
- **Ship/Rcv separate — TRUE.** Two physically separate dock clusters on
  different building faces (NE and SW) — consistent with separated receiving and
  shipping operations.
