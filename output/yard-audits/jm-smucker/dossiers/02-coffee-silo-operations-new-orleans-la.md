## Location confirmation

The roster pin (29.914282, -90.114356, ROOFTOP) landed inside container stacks
of the Port of New Orleans Napoleon Avenue Container Terminal — not on the
Silocaf building itself. Web research established that Silocaf ("the world's
largest automated silo plant for green-coffee processing") was built in the
early 1990s on the cleared site of the former Public Grain Elevator, at 5240
Coffee Dr inside the port terminal.

Searching the satellite imagery for the distinctive silo cluster located the
facility ~190 m SE of the pin: a compact vertical industrial building with a
visible **row of cylindrical bulk-storage silos** along its south edge,
processing conveyors, and a blue-roofed annex. This matches a green-coffee silo
plant and is the correct building. Locked center: 29.9139, -90.1122.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — TRUE (indirect).** The Silocaf building has no separate fenced
  perimeter or its own gate. It is inside the secured Port of New Orleans
  Napoleon Avenue Container Terminal; all truck traffic is controlled at the
  port terminal's manned gatehouse. Treated as a controlled truck entrance at
  the terminal level. Flagged uncertain — the gate belongs to the port, not the
  facility.
- **Guard shack — TRUE (inferred).** Port of New Orleans container terminals run
  manned gatehouses with credential/TWIC checks. Not directly visible (no public
  Street View inside the terminal); inferred from standard port-security
  practice. Flagged uncertain.
- **Multi-step — TRUE (inferred).** Port context implies a first checkpoint at
  the terminal gate, then a separate check-in / transfer point at the Silocaf
  building for bulk-coffee loading/unloading. Flagged uncertain.
- **Docks — "0-10" band.** This is a vertical silo plant, not a flat DC. Bulk
  green coffee moves via hopper trailers, conveyors and pneumatic transfer
  rather than a long rhythm of dock doors; ~6 transfer/dock points estimated.
  Flagged low confidence.
