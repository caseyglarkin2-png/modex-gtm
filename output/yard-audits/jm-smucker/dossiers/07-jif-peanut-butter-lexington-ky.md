- **Truck gate — TRUE.** The plant is a fully fenced urban complex. Street View
  along the internal access road off Winchester Rd (2024-2025) shows perimeter
  fencing and rolling chain-link gates at access points (one visible open at the
  rail crossing). Trucks pass through a controlled gated point.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed guard booth was identified at the truck
  driveway despite checking multiple Street View headings. Office-entrance
  signage reads "NO TRACTOR TRAILERS," routing trucks to a separate gate; that
  gate is a rolling fence gate with no booth visible. Flagged uncertain.
- **Remote GS — TRUE (uncertain).** A controlled truck gate exists with no guard
  shack found, implying kiosk / call-box / app check-in.
- **Docks:** Multiple building faces each show ~12-14 trailers backed in; total
  estimated in the 25-50 band across the ~28-acre, multi-building site.
- **Drop yard — TRUE.** Large dedicated trailer-storage lots on the
  west/northwest, 80+ trailers visible, capacity well above 50.
