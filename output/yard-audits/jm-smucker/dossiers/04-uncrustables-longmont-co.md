# Deep-Audit Dossier — Uncrustables, Longmont CO (idx 04)

**Account:** J.M. Smucker
**Facility:** Uncrustables manufacturing plant (2nd Uncrustables plant)
**Type:** Manufacturing — Frozen handhelds (Uncrustables)
**Address:** Concepts Industrial Park, Hwy 119 & Fairview St, Longmont, CO 80504
(roster listed 11555 N 75th St — see below)
**Resolved coordinates:** 40.16400, -105.05250

## Location confirmation

The roster pin (40.184331, -105.178241) was geocoded RANGE_INTERPOLATED off the
address 11555 N 75th St — but that address resolves to rural lake/farmland in NW
Longmont, with no industrial plant present. Web research established the actual
Smucker Uncrustables plant location: Smucker bought land in the **Concepts
Industrial Park near the NW corner of Colorado Highway 119 and Fairview
Street**, on the east end of Longmont in Weld County (~$4.65M land purchase).
The plant opened July 2019 ($340-350M, originally ~430,000 sq ft; a ~240,000 sq
ft expansion has since brought it to ~674,000 sq ft).

Satellite probing of the Hwy 119 / Fairview St area located a large white
food-plant building (~40.164, -105.0525) with rooftop solar, extensive dock
banks, and a large trailer drop yard. Street View (2024) confirmed the building
and the J.M. Smucker entrance sign on Fairview St. Locked center: 40.16400,
-105.05250.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — FALSE.** The main entrance off Fairview St is a wide OPEN paved
  driveway — no barrier arm, sliding gate or checkpoint pinch-point at the road
  (confirmed in 2024 Street View). The truck route runs in from the open
  driveway and loops around the south of the building to the dock yard. No
  controlled gate at the property line.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No guard booth at the entrance or along the frontage
  in Street View or satellite.
- **Remote check-in — FALSE.** No gate, so the flag does not apply.
- **Docks — "25-50" band (~45 doors est.).** Extensive dock banks on the east
  building face with many trailers backed in. Flagged low confidence (some dock
  faces partly shadowed).
- **Drop yard — TRUE, "50+" band.** Large dedicated trailer-storage lot on the
  NE holding dozens of parked trailers in marked rows; ~60 trailers visible,
  capacity ~90 — distinct from the active dock apron.
- **Ship/Rcv separate — FALSE.** Dock activity reads as one integrated bank on
  the east face.

## Web findings

- Opened July 31 2019; $340-350M investment; originally 430,000 sq ft.
- ~240,000 sq ft expansion (begun 2020) → ~674,000 sq ft total.
- Named 2020 Food Engineering Plant of the Year; described as the largest
  single-line bakery in the world at opening; ~560 employees.
- Doubled Uncrustables production previously concentrated at Scottsville KY;
  capacity up to ~420M sandwiches/year.

estimates.
