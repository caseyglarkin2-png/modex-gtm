# Deep-Audit Dossier — R.W. Knudsen Juices, Chico CA (idx 09)

## Facility
- **Account:** JM Smucker (roster) — **NOTE: divested, see below**
- **Type:** Manufacturing — juices / beverages (Knudsen)
- **Address:** 29-37 Speedway Ave, Chico, CA 95928
- **Resolved center:** 39.70670, -121.80250

## IMPORTANT — divestiture
This site is **no longer a J.M. Smucker facility**. Smucker sold its natural and
organic beverage and grains businesses — including the R.W. Knudsen brand and
the Chico manufacturing/distribution plant — to Nexus Capital Management for
$110 million; the transaction closed **January 31, 2022**. The plant now
operates under the **TruRoots Company / R.W. Knudsen Family**. The entrance sign
in 2024 Street View reads "TruRoots — Shipping / Receiving, 29 Speedway." The
roster's own source note flagged this status to confirm; it is hereby confirmed
divested. The physical yard audit below still stands for the operating juice
plant.

plant.
