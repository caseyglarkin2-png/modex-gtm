## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster coordinates (40.847437, -81.762353, ROOFTOP, 52 m move) landed on the
Smucker corporate campus. Satellite probes at z15–z20 confirmed a sprawling
multi-building industrial campus at the edge of Orrville: heavily landscaped
corporate HQ on the west side off Strawberry Lane, a large
manufacturing/distribution complex in the center-east, original Orrville plant
buildings to the north, and warehouse/aggregate-yard structures to the SE. Web
search confirmed One Strawberry Lane as Smucker HQ + a major manufacturing site.
The center coordinate was shifted east to the manufacturing/distribution complex
(the truck-relevant building), not the HQ office.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — FALSE.** Street View of the south truck driveway (Apr-2023 and
  Jan-2025) shows the truck yard opening directly onto the public road as a wide,
  open driveway. No barrier arm, no sliding/swing gate, no checkpoint
  pinch-point, no lane markings. The drop yard and dock area are reached through
  an uncontrolled entrance.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed booth at the south entrance; only a small
  free-standing yard sign post. No guard-booth-footprint structure at any campus
  truck access point.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** No controlled truck gate exists, so remote check-in
  does not apply.
- **Docks:** Main dock bank on the east face of the manufacturing building shows
  ~14 trailers backed in. Additional dock faces on other building sides put the
  campus total in the 25-50 band (estimate from overhead imagery — flagged
  uncertain).
- **Drop yard — TRUE.** Multiple dedicated trailer-storage rows south/southeast
  of the dock bank, well over 50 trailers of capacity.

## Yard zones and counts

- **Truck gate zone:** Open south driveway off the public road.
- **Drop yards:** Two boxed areas — the large south trailer lot and a smaller

- **Metrics:** ~38 dock doors (estimate), ~70 trailers visible, ~110 trailer
