## Step 0 — Location confirmation
Roster coordinates (41.003984, -79.327017, GEOMETRIC_CENTER, 4 m move) sat on
Keck Ave. Web search (Redbank Valley Chamber of Commerce, Yelp) confirmed the
J.M. Smucker peanut butter plant at 300 Keck Ave — originally an 1800s brewery,
later a milk plant, converted to peanut butter (Kitchen King) in 1946 and bought
by Smucker in 1965; one of three Smucker peanut butter facilities, ~60
employees, producing Smucker's, Adam's, Laura Scudder's and Santa Cruz peanut
butter. Satellite probes located the plant ~250 m southeast of the roster point,
a compact older multi-building industrial cluster between Keck/Penn-area
residential streets and Broad St (Rt 28) / Redbank Creek.
