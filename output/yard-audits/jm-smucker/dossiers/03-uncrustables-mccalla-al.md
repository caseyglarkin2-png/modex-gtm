## Location confirmation

The roster pin (33.29206, -87.082404) was geocoded APPROXIMATE and 5963 m off —
it landed in rural woods near I-20, not on the plant. Web research established
the plant address as 7351 Old Tuscaloosa Highway inside the **JeffMet McCalla**
(Jefferson Metropolitan Park) industrial park — a 739-acre park on the south
side of US-11, bordered by Old Tuscaloosa Hwy/US-11 to the NW and Norfolk
Southern Railroad to the SE, connected to I-20/59 via McAshan Drive. Smucker
bought ~225 acres there; the $1.1B, ~900,000 sq ft plant opened Nov 2024 as the
largest single-line bakery in the US.

Satellite probing of the JeffMet park located a single very large modern
food-plant building (~33.2992, -87.0595): a complex roofline, banks of rooftop
refrigeration units (consistent with a frozen-product plant), extensive dock
banks, truck yards, a large employee parking lot, and a controlled gated
entrance. Scale, frozen-plant roof signature and US-11 frontage all match the
Smucker Uncrustables plant. Locked center: 33.29920, -87.05950.
