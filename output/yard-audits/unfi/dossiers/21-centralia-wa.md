## Location resolution

The roster coordinates (46.732214, -123.009518, ROOFTOP geocode) landed
directly on the DC building. Web research confirms the site: UNFI's Pacific
Northwest Distribution Center at 4002 Galvin Rd is a **1.15 million sq ft,
state-of-the-art DC built on a ~77-acre site**, centrally located between
Seattle and Portland, leased long-term and built exclusively for UNFI
(Benaroya / UNFI press materials). It became operational H2 2019 and absorbed
the closing Tacoma, Auburn and Portland warehouses. The dossier confirms
Centralia received automation upgrades. Located in the Port of Centralia
industrial park; adjacent buildings to the NE are separate tenants.

## Gate / guard-shack determination

- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Street View (captured 2026-03) along the public loop
  road shows the entire dock yard enclosed by **chain-link perimeter fence**
  with a yellow-painted curb. The truck access drive crosses the fence line
  through a controlled vehicular gate; a **"SPEED LIMIT 15"** sign stands at
  the entry. This is a clear controlled checkpoint pinch-point.
- **Guard shack — FALSE (uncertain).** No staffed guard booth is visible in the
  truck lane in current satellite or Street View imagery. The small covered
  structures near the entry road resolve as an employee break shelter / transit
  amenity (picnic-table objects beside it), not a checkpoint booth. UNFI's
  corporate procedure at other DCs uses guard houses (a first house for UNFI
  drivers, a second shack for outside carriers past the fuel pumps), so a booth
  may exist on-site but it is **not visually confirmed at Centralia** — flagged
  uncertain.
- **Remote GS — TRUE.** Given a controlled gate but no confirmed booth,
  check-in is most plausibly kiosk/intercom-based. Verify in discovery.
- **Multi-step — FALSE.** No second checkpoint (scale house / second booth)
  visible after the gate.

confirmed. The guard-shack call is the soft spot — no booth is visible in
imagery, but UNFI's known corporate procedure means a staffed checkpoint cannot

overhead-imagery estimates flagged as uncertain.
