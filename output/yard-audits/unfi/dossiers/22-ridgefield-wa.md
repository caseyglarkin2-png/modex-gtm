**Type:** Distribution Center (natural / organic grocery wholesale)
**Resolved location:** 45.80660, -122.66585 — Ridgefield, Clark County WA
(Portland/Vancouver metro, edge of town)
**Confidence:** Medium

## Location resolution

The roster supplied "7909 S Union Ridge Pkwy, Ridgefield WA 98642" with
coordinates 45.805563, -122.66348. Probing satellite at the supplied point
landed directly on a very large distribution building. Waze and TruckMap both
list 7909 S Union Ridge Pkwy as the UNFI Ridgefield Distribution Center, and
2025-09 Street View confirms a posted "TRUCK ENTRANCE" sign at the property
frontage. Web research confirms UNFI's Ridgefield DC was a 232,000 sq ft
facility expanded by 541,000 sq ft (to ~800,000 sq ft total) as part of UNFI's
2019 Pacific Northwest distribution-network optimization. Locked center:
45.80660, -122.66585.

A separate large logistics facility with a distinctive colorful (orange/green)
trailer fleet sits immediately NW across the road — a different operator,
excluded from the UNFI perimeter.
