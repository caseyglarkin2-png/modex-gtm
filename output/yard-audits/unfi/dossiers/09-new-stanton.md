## Location resolution

The roster supplied a city-level address ("100 UNFI Way, Hunker, PA") flagged as
approximate. Web research resolved the facility to the legacy SuperValu / UNFI
Pittsburgh-division distribution center at **400 Paintersville Rd, New Stanton,
PA 15672**. Sources note the Paintersville Rd address is the **auto entrance
only** and that the **truck entrance is signed off 1769 New Stanton Ruffsdale
Rd, Ruffs Dale, PA** — i.e., a single shared access corridor approaches the
site, with trucks routed within it.

The roster coordinates (40.212001, -79.611695) landed directly on the building
roof, confirming the right property without a move. Satellite at zoom 16-18
shows an unambiguous large multi-temperature grocery distribution complex
immediately south of the I-70 / Pennsylvania Turnpike New Stanton interchange.

## What the imagery showed

- **Wide satellite (z16-17):** A large DC complex bounded by Sewickley Creek and
  an active rail line on the north/east, woods on the west, and employee
  parking on the SE. The site comprises a **main DC building** and a **long
  separate dock building on the west side**.
- **Access:** A single access road leaves the highway-interchange roundabout,
  crosses a bridge over Sewickley Creek, and enters the property. It forks
  inside the property — autos to the SE employee lot, trucks toward the dock
  yards.
- **Street View (entrance, captured 2024-10):** Panos only cover the public
  bridge segment. The SW-facing view shows **"ALL TRUCK TRAFFIC KEEP RIGHT"**
  and **"SPEED LIMIT 15"** signage at the entrance, with trailers visible
  toward the facility. This confirms a **controlled, truck-routed industrial
  entrance**, but Street View does not penetrate the property line, so the gate
  structure itself (barrier arm vs. open) is not directly visible.
- **Docks:** The west building face carries a long, regular bank of dock doors
  with many trailers backed in. The main building shows additional dock banks
  on its south and north faces. Total estimate ~95 dock doors (50+ band).
- **Drop yard:** A large trailer staging lot on the NW holds 50+ trailers;
  additional angled trailer drop runs along the north building edge.
- **Rail:** An active rail line runs the north/east edge with a spur into the
  building — the site is rail-served.
