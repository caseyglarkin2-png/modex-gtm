## Location confirmation
The roster coordinates landed directly on a long rectangular distribution
warehouse in the Apollo Dr industrial park on the northwest edge of Champaign.
Web search confirms 3001 Apollo Dr as the UNFI / W. Newell & Co. distribution
center (legacy SuperValu / W. Newell conventional-grocery DC, UNFI Central
region), with a posted driver receiving window of roughly 1 AM–noon. Street
View from the access road shows a building-mounted "UNFI" sign and a "UNFI
Distribution / Office / Visitor" directional marker, positively identifying
the site.

## Gate / guard determination
- **Truck gate: NO.** Every probe of the access roads and the truck-yard
  approaches shows open, uncontrolled pavement. No barrier arm, no sliding
  or swing gate, no checkpoint pinch-point with lane markings where the
  property meets the road. Truck driveways flow straight off the shared
  industrial-park road onto the truck yard.
- **Guard shack: NO.** No booth structure (1–3-vehicle footprint) at any
  entrance. The only roadside structure is a low UNFI directional sign.
- **Remote GS: NO** — there is no gate, so a kiosk/call-box check-in flag
  does not apply.
- This is the textbook open-campus profile for a legacy SuperValu / W.
  Newell conventional-grocery DC in a mid-sized Midwest industrial park.
