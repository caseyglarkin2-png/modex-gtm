## Step 0 — Location confirmation (significant correction)
The roster coordinate (40.094265, -93.603911, RANGE_INTERPOLATED) and the
roster's stated street address were both wrong — the roster note itself warns
"street address approximate - exact street not confirmed." Probes around the
roster point found only farmland, residential streets, a school, and a grain
co-op — no large food plant.

Web research established the plant's real address as **1401 Harris Ave**
(Waze, Manta, LoopNet APN 110516030601100) — the historic Carnation / Trenton
Foods / ConAgra Grocery Foods vienna-sausage plant Nestlé bought from Conagra
in 2018 for Chef-mate production. The correct building was located ~1.5 km SW
of the roster point: a large multi-section industrial plant with a rail yard
on its west side and employee parking on its east. It was **positively
confirmed** by a 2023 Street View of the east face showing a "Nestlé
PROFESSIONAL — Making MORE Possible" sign over an Employee Entrance door.
Locked center 40.08080, -93.61050.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — FALSE (uncertain).** This is an older urban-fabric plant.
  The SW/S truck yard reads as open pavement continuous with the adjacent
  street; tractors and trailers park freely with no barrier arm or pinch-point
  visible in 2023 Street View. Some perimeter chain-link exists but the truck
  yard entrance is uncontrolled in the imagery. Flagged uncertain.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed booth at any truck entrance. A small
  structure existed near the SW in 2008 imagery but does not read as a guard
  booth on the truck lane.
- **Docks — 10-25 band.** Modest dock count typical of a canning plant; doors
  with trailers backed in on the W and SW faces. Low confidence on exact count.
- **Drop yard — 10-25 band, dropYard true.** NE-corner trailer lot, ~15-20
  trailers.
- **Rail-served — TRUE.** A multi-track rail yard runs immediately along the
  plant's west boundary with siding into the plant.

## Web findings
- Historic Carnation / Trenton Foods → ConAgra Grocery Foods vienna-sausage
  plant; Trenton was once "the world's largest producer of vienna sausages."
  Conagra announced closure (282 jobs); Nestlé bought the factory + the
  proprietary 1964 cooked-before-canning line in 2018 for Chef-mate (sausage
  gravy, chili con carne, cheese sauce). Nestlé Professional later announced a
  $7.5M expansion (~30+ jobs). (PR Newswire, Republican-Times, MO DED, KSHB,
  Powder & Bulk Solids.)

## Final confidence: MEDIUM
The facility was positively located and confirmed (Nestlé signage), and the
physical layout is well-imaged. The roster's coordinates/address were wrong and
had to be corrected. Gate/guard-shack are confidently absent in imagery but the
open-yard read and exact dock count are flagged uncertain.
