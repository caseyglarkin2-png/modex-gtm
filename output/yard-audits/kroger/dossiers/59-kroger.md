## Resolved location
- **Confirmed building:** Kroger Cincinnati Fresh Center (Cincinnati Fresh Distribution Center), 6300 Creek Rd, Blue Ash, OH 45242.
- **Center:** ~39.26280, -84.36280. Supplied approximate coordinates (39.2625, -84.362738) were accurate.
- **How confirmed:** Satellite z16-18 around the supplied point shows a single ~400,000 sq ft perishable warehouse. Web search corroborates a 390k-400k SF Kroger perishable DC at 6300 Creek Rd (LEO A DALY project page, TruckMap, Birdeye "The Kroger Co. Cincinnati Fresh Center"). Street View confirms "Kroger" branding on the building and on parked trailers in the west drop yard.

## Setting
Suburban industrial park in Blue Ash (Cincinnati metro), I-71 to the east, residential to the north, other warehouses adjacent south/west. Dense metro fabric -> **Urban**. Not isolated -> no connectivity issue.

## Gate / guard / fence determinations
- **Perimeter fence:** Continuous chain-link fence rings the property (confirmed in multiple Street View frames along Creek Rd and at the Carver Rd frontage).
- **Truck gate (TRUE):** Posted sign at the gated Carver Rd entrance reads "VEHICLE ENTRANCE ONLY / TRUCKS ENTER OFF CREEK RD". The passenger gate is a sliding chain-link gate; trucks are routed to a separate Creek Rd gate. This is a controlled, gated entry.
- **Guard shack (FALSE) / remoteGs (TRUE):** No clearly staffed guard booth identifiable at either gate in available imagery. A small structure near the SW yard edge could be a gatehouse but is unconfirmed; treated as remote/kiosk check-in. Flagged uncertain.
- **Entry/exit separate (TRUE):** Signage explicitly separates the passenger-vehicle entrance (Carver Rd) from the truck entrance (Creek Rd).
