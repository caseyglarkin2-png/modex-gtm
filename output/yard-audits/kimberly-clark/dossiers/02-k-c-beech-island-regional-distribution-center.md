## Step 0 — Location confirmation: NOT RESOLVABLE (facility not yet built)

The roster coordinates point to undeveloped forest and active residential
subdivision construction north-east of the K-C Beech Island plant — no
distribution center exists there. Probes at z16–z18 across the surrounding area
show only:
- Residential subdivisions under construction (single-family homes).
- Small commercial / contractor buildings along Old Jackson Hwy.
- Forest, and the north building edge of the existing Beech Island plant.

Web research resolves the discrepancy: this DC is **an expansion of the
existing Beech Island plant**, not a separate site. SC Governor's office, SC Biz
News, and Manufacturing Dive report Kimberly-Clark will **add 1.1 million
square feet** of automated distribution space **at 246 Old Jackson Highway**
(the same address as the plant, roster idx 1). The project is a $200M
investment announced May 2025, **projected to be completed and operational in
Q2 2027**, adding ~150 jobs with robotics and AI-powered logistics.

As of the current (2026) satellite imagery, the DC has **not been built** — no
structure, dock face, drop yard, or truck gate exists to audit.
