# Stop & Shop — FOV verification rejections

Verification run: 2026-06-19 (agent, real web research per `scripts/yard-audit/verify-facility-prompt.md`).

**Result: 0 rejections out of 7 sites. All 7 confirmed currently operating.**

Stop & Shop is an Ahold Delhaize USA banner; distribution was brought in-house from
3PL C&S Wholesale Grocers into Ahold's own arm, **ADUSA Supply Chain / ADUSA
Distribution**. The direction of travel across the network is *toward* ADUSA
self-distribution, not divestiture. Six of seven sites are ADUSA self-operated
(C&S now appears only as a residual third-party labor-management vendor at the
former-C&S sites). The seventh (Plainville) is operated by 3PL **Americold** under
a 20-year design-build-operate agreement for ADUSA.

Closure/divestiture gauntlet (closed/closing, WARN layoff, consolidation,
sold/transferred/idled, reverted-to-C&S, bankruptcy-era) was run on every site.
Two recent labor events surfaced but **neither is a closure** — both documented
below as resolved/misread negatives.

## Resolved/misread negatives (flagged, not rejected)
- **Freetown (01 + 02) — Feb 2025 closure THREAT, RESOLVED.** Stop & Shop publicly
  warned it would shutter the Freetown warehouse and revert to 3PL distribution
  ~June 2025 absent a Teamsters Local 25 deal. A six-year contract was reached
  2025-02-28 preserving 900+ jobs; the DC stays open and in-house. No WARN, sale,
  or idling post-deal. [Tier 2: https://wbsm.com/freetown-stop-shop-teamsters-contract/ , 2025-02-28]
  *Re-check flag:* most recent scare, ~16 months old — worth a re-verify down the road.
- **Manchester (03) — Feb 2024 WARN was a 3PL transition, NOT a DC closure.** The
  WARN (Freight Handlers LLC, 383 jobs, eff. 2024-03-31) was the prior 3PL
  contractor ending; ADUSA stated it "will continue to operate the Manchester
  distribution center" and brought labor in-house. Strengthens operator=self.
  [Tier 2: https://www.nbcconnecticut.com/news/local/freight-handlers-manchester-facility-to-close-in-march/3211811/ , 2024-02]
