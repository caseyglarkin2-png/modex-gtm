## Location confirmation
The roster pin (34.870596, -82.032427, ROOFTOP) landed on a large white-roof
tilt-up warehouse along Moore Duncan Hwy (SC-290). Web research confirms this is
**Building 500 at Tyger River Industrial Park (TRIP)**, the spec building where
Keurig Dr Pepper leased ~203,000 sq ft in September 2024 for next-generation
K-Rounds (plastic-free coffee pod) production as part of a $141M Spartanburg
County expansion (SC Dept of Commerce / SC Governor / Colliers releases). June
2025 Street View shows the completed building still carrying a Colliers
"for lease" leasing sign at the highway frontage, consistent with a recently
occupied spec building. The building runs NW-SE, roughly 600 ft long.

A separate large building with heavy dock/trailer activity sits to the SE on a
different parcel within the same park — it is NOT this facility and was excluded.
