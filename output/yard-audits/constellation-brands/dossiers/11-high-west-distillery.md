## Web findings
High West Distillery, Utah's first legal distillery since 1870, opened its
Wanship distillery and tasting room in 2015 on the Blue Sky Ranch. Access is via
the Blue Sky Ranch gatehouse; all visitors park at the base of the ranch and
shuttle up to the distillery. High West is a craft spirits brand retained in
Constellation's portfolio. The site is a production distillery plus hospitality
(refectory/restaurant, general store) — craft-scale, not a high-throughput
freight DC.

## Final confidence
Medium. The distillery complex was positively located, and the production
layout and loading apron are clear from z20-z21 satellite. Confidence is held at
medium because the truckGate / guardShack calls rest on High West's official
description of a Blue Sky Ranch gatehouse rather than direct imagery (no Street
View on the private road), and the gatehouse serves the whole ranch resort
rather than the distillery dock specifically. Those fields, the door count, and
the connectivity inference are flagged uncertain.
