## Location confirmation (Step 0)

The roster coordinate (38.434323, -122.400476, RANGE_INTERPOLATED, moved only
4 m) was placed precisely against the street address, but the address
interpolation landed in the **To Kalon vineyard** just SE of the actual built
campus. Satellite probing at zoom 16-21 located the real building complex
~150 m NW of the roster point, centered ~38.4357, -122.4013.

This is positively the **Robert Mondavi Winery** — the historic Napa Valley
estate winery in Oakville, anchored by the renowned To Kalon Vineyard, with
the iconic Cliff May Mission-style arch and bell tower. Web research confirms
the estate reopened to visitors on April 20, 2026 following a multi-year
transformation (PRNewswire / Constellation press releases). The estate sits on
the EAST side of CA-29 / St. Helena Highway, surrounded by vineyards.

  parking. No barrier arm, sliding/swing gate, or checkpoint pinch-point
  across the lane in Street View or satellite imagery. Open winery estate
  entrance.
- **Guard shack — false.** No staffed booth at the entrance — only a
  landscaped monument sign and the parking lot.
- **Remote GS — false.** No truck gate identified.
- **Docks — "0-10".** Estate winery, not a freight DC. No long bank of freight
  dock doors. A small service/loading area on the production-cellar building
  handles wine and grape movement; estimate ~3-7 service doors. Low confidence.
- **Drop area — NONE.** No marked trailer-parking stalls holding bobtailed
  trailers; this is not a drop yard.
