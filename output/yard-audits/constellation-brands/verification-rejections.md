# Constellation Brands — FOV Verification Rejections

## Rejected

- **Constellation Gonzales Winery** (Gonzales, CA — 800 S. Alta Street, 93926) — REJECTED:
  DIVESTED to The Wine Group in the June 2, 2025 wine transaction. This Monterey County
  bulk-wine production winery (historically made the mainstream brands — Woodbridge / Meiomi /
  Robert Mondavi Private Selection — that were sold) transferred with the "associated facilities
  and vineyards." The Wine Group's own newsroom names it explicitly: "its other new winemaking
  site is in Gonzales, about five miles north of its plant in Soledad, Monterey County."
  No longer Constellation-operated. The roster's "Retained after 2025 Wine Group sale" note was
  incorrect.
