## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster coordinates (geocoded to the 2989 Tucker St address, movedMeters 36)
sat on the airport apron just east of the Honda Aero building. Satellite search
identified the facility: a single large modern white-roof manufacturing building
on the west side of Burlington-Alamance Regional Airport, with an aircraft apron
facing the runway and employee parking on the Tucker St (landside) face.
Confirmed by:
- 2026 Street View along Tucker St showing the modern Honda Aero facility behind
  the airport perimeter fence.
- z17-z20 satellite showing the building, apron and parking layout.
- Web research: Honda Aero produces the HF120 engine for GE Honda Aero Engines;
  ~82,000 sq ft original plant plus a $21M / 50,000 sq ft expansion broken
  ground in 2016, completed ~2017 — roughly 132,000 sq ft total.

## Facility nature — important context
This is a jet-engine ASSEMBLY and test plant, not a freight/distribution
facility. Truck traffic is minimal (small inbound parts shipments). There are no
trailer drop yards and only a handful of service dock doors. The dominant "yard"
is the airside aircraft apron, which is not a truck yard.
