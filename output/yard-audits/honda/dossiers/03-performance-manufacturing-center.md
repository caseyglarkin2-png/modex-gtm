# Deep-Audit Dossier — Honda Performance Manufacturing Center (PMC)

**Facility:** Honda - Performance Manufacturing Center (PMC), Marysville OH
**Type:** Auto Assembly Plant (low-volume)
**Address:** 25000 Honda Parkway, Marysville, OH 43040
**Resolved center:** 40.27930, -83.52530
**Confidence:** Medium

## Location confirmation
Roster geocode (40.27761, -83.525972) was effectively rooftop-accurate (moved
26 m). Satellite probes confirm a single dark-roof main building with an
attached white-roof east annex. A user-contributed Street View interior photo
at the site shows the Acura NSX assembly floor — positively identifying this as
the Performance Manufacturing Center. Web research (hondanews, Gardner Web)
confirms PMC is a ~184,000 sq ft low-volume plant with ~100 associates, housed
in Honda's former North American Logistics facility, which built the NSX
2016-2022 and later the Acura Integra Type S.
