## Step 0 — Location correction
The roster geocode (36.161268, -86.690056) landed in an office park ("Atrium
Way" — multi-tenant office buildings), inconsistent with a 297,500 sq ft
production/distribution facility. Web research (Nashville Post, Berry Hill town
directory, Yelp) established the actual facility: Coca-Cola Bottling Co.
Consolidated, 407 Craighead St, Nashville TN 37204. Tennessee Soft Drink
Production Co. (a Coca-Cola Consolidated affiliate) owns this 21.76-acre
property and paid $56M for it in 2024. Satellite probes confirmed a large
white-roofed production/distribution building with dock doors, extensive
diagonal trailer parking, and a CSX rail line along the east edge. Audit
performed at the corrected location.
