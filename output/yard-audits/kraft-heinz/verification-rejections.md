# Kraft Heinz — FOV Verification Rejections

Per-site facility verification run 2026-06-19 (agent). 27 sites checked.
Result: 25 confirmed, 1 probable (flagged), 1 rejected.

The divestiture/closure gauntlet (cheese -> Lactalis 2021, Planters -> Hormel 2021,
2024-2025 plant closures + WARN, the paused 2025-26 KHC split) was run on every site.
Notably, NO cheese plant fell to the Lactalis natural-cheese divestiture — every
cheese-adjacent site in this roster is a RETAINED KHC brand or has converted:

- **Beaver Dam, WI (16)** — Philadelphia Cream Cheese, explicitly RETAINED by KHC (Lactalis deal was Tulare CA / Walton NY / Wausau WI / Weyauwega WI only).
- **New Ulm, MN (23)** — Velveeta + Deli Deluxe, PROCESSED cheese, never in the natural-cheese sale.
- **Lowville, NY (22)** — ended string cheese Nov 2024 but CONVERTED to KHC Philadelphia Cream Cheese; still KHC, 500+ employees.
- **Springfield, MO (11)** — Kraft Mac & Cheese + American Singles, RETAINED.
- **Champaign, IL (18)** — cheese lines MOVED OUT but the plant stayed open as a KHC dry/sauce center-of-excellence.
- **Kendallville, IN (21)** — cheese ties ended in 1995; now Jet-Puffed marshmallows.
- **Avon, NY (24)** — Cool Whip + Lunchables, not natural cheese.

No Planters/Hormel exposure: the only nut-adjacent suspicion (Fort Myers) is a Bagel Bites frozen-snacks plant, not a Planters site.
