# Ball - Golden CO — Beverage Can Plant

**Address:** 4525 Indiana St, Golden, CO 80403
**Resolved center:** 39.777300, -105.167200
**Confidence:** high

## Verification (Step -1) — CONFIRMED
Ball's oldest beverage can plant, operating on this site since 1962 (originally Jeffco
Manufacturing). Four can-manufacturing lines; produces 8/12/16/24-oz bodies plus ends
and a CapCan line; ~95M cans/day; ~360 employees. Corroborated by the Golden Chamber
member listing at 4525 Indiana St and a 2024 Ball-hosted Women in Manufacturing factory
tour of the plant. Ran the closure gauntlet: this site is NOT among Ball's 2022-2024
North American plant closures (Phoenix, St. Paul MN, Wallkill NY, Kent WA, Verona VA
slug plant). Company-operated, owned. `verdict = confirmed`.

## Step 0 — Building confirmation
Supplied coordinates landed on the plant. Wide satellite (z16-17) confirmed a single
large L-shaped manufacturing complex: a wide main block on the east and a long west
wing running parallel to a rail corridor along the north edge. Reservoir to the NW,
municipal ball fields and residential to the east, an E-W public frontage road on the
south. Employee parking sits on the east side; truck/dock activity is on the south and
southwest faces.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **truckGate: FALSE.** The operational truck interface is the open south dock apron —
  trucks back into "EAST DOCKS" directly from the public road with no barrier arm,
  sliding gate, or checkpoint. Perimeter is only partially chain-link fenced with
  sliding yard gates on the west drive; the docks themselves are uncontrolled.
- **guardShack: FALSE / remoteGs: FALSE.** No staffed booth anywhere along the
  frontage (checked E/W/SE/SW panos, 2019-2025). No gate ⇒ remoteGs false.
- **dockDoors: 25-50 (modeled).** Long dock line on the south face; ~5-6 trailers
  visible at capture; band estimated for a 4-line, ~95M-can/day plant.
- **dropArea / dropYard: 25-50 / true.** Freight-trailer rows staged NW between the
  plant and the rail (~25-40 trailers).
