## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster coordinate sits squarely on a very large rectangular distribution
warehouse with rooftop solar panels. Web research (Yelp, Manta, Foursquare,
Yellowpages, CMac) all list the **Cost Plus World Market Stockton Distribution
Center at 3610 S Airport Way, Stockton CA 95206**, and a Kaleris case study
names it as the company's West Coast DC. A 500,000 sq ft DC was built for Cost
Plus World Market by Ellis-Gibson Development — consistent with the footprint
seen here. The roster geocode (ROOTOP, moved 2,396 m) landed correctly on the
building. The truck gate is separately listed as "Cost Plus World Market Truck
Gate" at 1735 Zephyr St on Waze, with operating hours Mon-Sat 07:00-16:00.
Building positively identified.

## What the key views showed

- **Roster point z18-19:** The DC is one big rectangle, rooftop solar. Dock

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — TRUE.** A named, hours-managed "Cost Plus World Market Truck
  Gate" business exists (1735 Zephyr St, Waze). The whole property is enclosed:
  a continuous block/concrete perimeter wall plus hedge along Zephyr St (seen in
  multiple Street View captures), and chain-link fence around the north and
  south trailer yards. The gate itself sits set back inside the property.
- **Guard shack — FALSE (low confidence).** No guard-booth structure (the small
  1-3-vehicle-footprint booth with windows) was visible in any Street View pano
  along Zephyr St — that frontage is unbroken wall — nor at the open office/
  visitor driveway on the east side. No booth identified at any entrance.
- **Remote GS — TRUE.** Because a controlled, named truck gate exists but no
  guard shack could be confirmed, a kiosk / call-box / app check-in is implied.
- **Dock doors — 50+.** Dock doors line both long faces of the building (north
  and south), each face well over 200 m, with trailers backed in. Combined
  estimate ~90 doors.
- **Ship/Rcv separate — TRUE (medium).** Two distinct dock banks on opposite
  building faces suggest physically separate shipping vs. receiving.

## Yard zones and counts
- **Perimeter:** Encloses the building, the north drop yard, and the large

- **Drop yards:** (1) north of the building, (2) NE angled-stall lot,
  (3) the large south drop yard across the rail.
- **Dock aprons:** north dock apron and south dock apron.
- **Staging:** trucks stage along Zephyr St outside the gate (pre-gate);
  internal paved holding inside the gate (post-gate).
- **yardMetrics:** ~90 dock doors, ~240 trailers visible, ~320 trailer

## Web findings

## Final confidence
**Medium.** The building, docks, drop yards and rail are clear and high-
confidence. The truck gate is confirmed by a named business listing, but the
guard-shack determination is the weak point: Street View shows continuous wall
along the public-road frontage with no visible booth, so the guard-shack /
remote-GS call rests on inference. Gate-lane counts are estimates.
