## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (ROOFTOP geocode, moved only 8 m) landed directly on a
very large rectangular distribution warehouse set in open farmland off US Route
460. Web research (Nextdoor, CMac, Foursquare, TruckMap, Isle of Wight Chamber
of Commerce, and a Cost Plus press release) all confirm the **Cost Plus World
Market Virginia Distribution Center at 12300 Dominion Way, Windsor VA 23487**.
A "World Market" monument sign is visible at the entrance driveway in Street
View. Building positively identified — the roster point is correct.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Trucks use a dedicated entrance separate from the
  "NO TRUCKS" car driveway. The truck route runs to the SE corner of the
  building to a controlled gate / pinch-point.
- **Guard shack — TRUE (high confidence).** A small rectangular guard-booth
  structure is clearly visible at z20 on a triangular paved island at the SE
  building corner, the truck lane splitting around it. This is corroborated by
  TruckMap driver notes which state plainly: **"Check-in is at the security
  guard shack."** Driver notes also reference appointment-only receiving, door
  17 / door 41, driver restrooms near door 41, and no overnight parking.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** A staffed guard shack is present.
- **Dock doors — 50+.** Dock doors run the full length of both long faces
  (north and south), each face ~600 m, with trailers backed in. Driver notes
  reference high door numbers (door 41+). Combined estimate ~110 doors.
- **Ship/Rcv separate — TRUE (medium).** Two distinct dock banks on opposite
  building faces suggest separate shipping and receiving operations.
