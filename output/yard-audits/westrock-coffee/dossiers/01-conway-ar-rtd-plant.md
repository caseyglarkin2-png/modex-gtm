## Resolved location

The roster supplied 35.073662, -92.416857 (ROOFTOP geocode, but the pin landed
on the NE construction/laydown corner of the property). Web research confirmed
this is the **former Kimberly-Clark Conway paper-products plant at 480 Exchange
Avenue**, which Westrock Coffee purchased and converted into North America's
largest roast-to-RTD beverage manufacturing facility — a $315M project, ~570,000
sq ft on a ~45-acre campus, grand opening June 2024 (Arkansas Business, Dairy
Foods, Westrock Coffee press release). Satellite probing confirmed a large
sprawling multi-section industrial complex consistent with a converted paper
mill. The center was locked to the main building mass at 35.0721, -92.4188.

## Web findings

Former Kimberly-Clark Conway plant (524,000 sq ft purchased; expanded to ~570K).
$315M roast-to-RTD conversion; opened June 2024; up to 850-900 jobs at full
capacity. Largest roast-to-RTD facility in North America — canned/bottled cold
brew, teas, juice products, single-serve cups, plus a beverage-development lab.
