## Resolved location

The roster supplied 35.058779, -92.409215 — that pin landed ~350m too far west,
on an older orange-roofed trucking/logistics yard unrelated to Westrock. Web
research established the "Clark" facility is a 525,000 sq ft single-serve
(K-cup pod) plant on William J. Clark Drive — 130,000 sq ft of manufacturing
plus fulfillment/distribution — that Westrock opened in July 2025 (Westrock
Coffee press release, Talk Business & Politics, Food Processing).

Working the William J. Clark Drive corridor: the **DC ("1430")** is the
north white-roof building (confirmed by the "1430" facade in Street View); the
large **gray-roof warehouse/manufacturing building immediately SOUTH** of the DC
is the Clark plant. Its footprint and trailer activity match a 525K sq ft
plant. The shell is already present in 2024 imagery — consistent with a
Tempus-developed building that Westrock fitted out and occupied operationally
in July 2025. Center re-locked to 35.0578, -92.4050.
