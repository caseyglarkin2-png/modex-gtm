## Resolved location
The roster geocode (ROOFTOP, moved 95 m) lands on the correct building. Web
search confirms "Westrock Coffee Company — Roasting & Manufacturing" at 30
Collins Industrial Pl, North Little Rock (Little Rock Chamber listing, Yelp,
Waze, joe.coffee, NAICS). This is Westrock's original/legacy private-label
roasting plant predating the new Conway campus.

The building is the white-roofed, L-shaped industrial structure on the south
side of Collins Industrial Place, inside the Collins Industrial Place
light-industrial subdivision. It is positively distinguished from neighbors by
its L footprint, its own front (north) employee/visitor parking apron, and the
roasting-plant dock arm on the southwest leg.
