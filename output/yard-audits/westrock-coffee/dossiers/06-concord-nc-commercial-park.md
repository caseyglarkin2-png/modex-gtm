## Resolved location
The roster geocode for this address (ROOFTOP) moved 1693 m, so the building was
verified independently rather than trusting the pin. Web search confirms S&D
Coffee & Tea at 101 Commercial Park Dr SW, Concord NC (Waze, BusinessYab,
Manta). Panjiva buyer records list an "S&D Tea Annex" at 101 Commercial Park
Drive SW — corroborating that this is a separate S&D/Westrock facility (an
extract/tea node), not the main campus.

The building is the large warehouse on Commercial Park Drive SW, inside the
Commercial Park Drive industrial subdivision, against a wooded edge to the south
and east. It is ~0.4 mi southwest of idx 5 (300 Concord Parkway South). The two
are confirmed distinct buildings: idx 5 is the multi-building roasting campus on
the parkway; idx 6 is a single warehouse on the Commercial Park loop. They were
not confused.
