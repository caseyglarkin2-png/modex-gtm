## LOCATION CONFIDENCE: LOW — flagged for human review
The roster address "1101 Willowbrook Road" does not resolve to any confirmed
Campbell's address. Web research firmly establishes the **Campbell's / Kettle
Foods Beloit potato-chip plant at 3150 Kettle Way** in Gateway Business Park —
a LEED-Gold plant (~73,000 sq ft per construction records; ~140,000 sq ft per a
Beloit economic-development page), ~300 employees, processing ~50M lbs of
potatoes per year, notable for **18 rooftop wind turbines**.

During the audit, the large process complex first examined (heavy silos, tanks,
process equipment at ~42.513, -88.991) was positively identified by Street View
signage as an **IFF (International Flavors & Fragrances)** facility — **not
Campbell's**. The audited candidate building (~42.5121, -88.9938) is the
best-matching alternative: a ~73,000 sq ft white-roof industrial building in
the same Gateway Business Park, consistent with the Kettle plant's documented
scale. However, Campbell's/Kettle signage could not be confirmed in the limited
Street View coverage, the documented rooftop wind turbines were not clearly
resolvable in available imagery, and the roster's "Willowbrook Road" address
mismatch was not reconciled. Identity is therefore LOW confidence.
