# Deep-Audit Dossier — GP Brunswick Cellulose Mill, Brunswick GA (idx 10)

## Resolved location
- **Locked center:** 31.1760, -81.5120
- **Address:** Mill on the Turtle River, 9th Street West area, Brunswick, GA
  31521 (roster lists 100 Pulp Plant Rd).
- The roster point (31.229422, -81.512762, GEOMETRIC_CENTER) was ~6 km N of the
  actual mill, in a residential/commercial part of Brunswick. Step-0 satellite
  + web research (mill is on the Turtle River) located the large pulp mill

  truckloads of yellow pine per day; subject of a $200M bio-refinery upgrade.
