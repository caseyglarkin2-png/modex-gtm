## Location & how it was confirmed

The roster geocode (44.60417, -123.935516; address "1 Butler Bridge Rd") landed
in the Yaquina River and wastewater-treatment-pond area south of the real mill —
imprecise, not on a building. Probing wider (z15) immediately revealed the
unmistakable Georgia-Pacific Toledo Mill complex on a peninsula on the east bank
of the Yaquina River, on the west side of the town of Toledo, Oregon. Web
research confirmed the address as 1400 SE Butler Bridge Rd, Toledo, OR 97391 and
the facility as GP's integrated kraft pulp and linerboard mill, operating since
1957. Center locked at ~44.6145, -123.9370.

The mill makes linerboard for corrugated boxes from virgin wood chips and
recycled fiber; GP also runs its Juno recycling-technology facility on-site. It
is the Infor Phase 2 dossier facility.

- **Street View (2024-07):** Continuous perimeter chain-link/red-mesh fencing
