# GP Monticello Mill — Monticello, MS — Deep-Audit Dossier

**Roster idx:** 14
**Facility type:** Containerboard Mill (integrated kraft pulp + linerboard)
**Resolved coordinates:** 31.6282, -90.0815
**Confidence:** High

## Location & how it was confirmed

The roster geocode (31.5795, -90.0394; address "1715 Highway 84 E") placed the
facility on Highway 84 in open pine forest roughly 5.6 km SSE of the real mill —
a city-level geocode error, not a usable pin. Web research (Global Energy Monitor
"Monticello Paper power station", CLUI Land Use Database) returned exact
coordinates 31.6282, -90.0815. Satellite imagery there shows an unmistakable
large integrated paper mill on Na Sandifer Hwy (Old Highway 84), on the Pearl
River about one mile east of Monticello in Lawrence County, MS. Center locked
there.

The facility is Georgia-Pacific's Monticello Mill, an integrated kraft pulp and
linerboard (containerboard) mill. In 2024 it became one of few North American
facilities to exceed 1 million tons of annual product. A $91M upgrade was
announced in 2023 (Infor Phase 2 per the account dossier).

## What the key views showed

- **Wide satellite (z15-16):** A full integrated mill campus — paper-machine
  buildings, two large radial log/chip storage piles in the woodyard, a
  wastewater treatment plant (circular clarifiers + multiple large ponds on the
  west), a finished-goods shipping warehouse on the NW side, a rail yard along
  the north edge, and many support structures. Pearl River borders the SW.
- **Mill core (z17-18):** Process buildings with steam plumes; two circular
  woodyard stacker/reclaimer log piles; conveyors; rail spurs.
- **Shipping warehouse (z17-19):** A large warehouse (white roof) on the NW side
  with a dock face and rows of dozens of parked trailers in an adjacent drop
  yard. Rail spurs and covered rail loading sheds serve the same warehouse zone.
- **Entrance / Street View (2024-03):** The single mill access road branches NE
  off Na Sandifer Hwy near 31.6213,-90.0805 and runs ~400 m into the property.
  Street View shows the mill roadside sign, the entrance road with a parked
  semi, and industrial structures ahead. Coverage stops at the private property
  line. Satellite of the access road shows trailers/trucks queued bumper-to-
  bumper along it and a small scale/guard-house-sized structure near
  31.6236,-90.0838.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **truckGate — TRUE.** A single controlled mill entrance: the access road off
  Na Sandifer Hwy funnels all truck traffic through one checkpoint. A controlled,
  gated and scaled entrance is universal for an integrated paper mill receiving
  log/chip vans; gate structures sit at the property line just beyond Street
  View coverage.
- **guardShack — TRUE (medium confidence).** A small structure consistent with a
  guard/scale house is visible at the entrance/scale area near 31.6236,-90.0838.
  Not resolved at high detail — flagged in uncertainFields.
- **remoteGs — FALSE.** A staffed guard/scale house is the expected and visible
  arrangement.
- **scale — TRUE (medium confidence).** Truck scales for chip/log van weigh-in
  are operationally required at a mill of this scale; a scale-house-sized
  structure sits in the truck path. Not pinpointed precisely — flagged.
- **dockDoors — "10-25".** The NW finished-goods warehouse shows a dock face with
  banks of doors and trailers backed in; ~18 estimated, medium confidence (roof
  shadow obscures exact count).
- **shipRcvSeparate — TRUE.** Receiving (woodyard log/chip vans, chemical and
  rail receiving) is physically separate from finished linerboard shipping at the
  NW warehouse dock bank.
