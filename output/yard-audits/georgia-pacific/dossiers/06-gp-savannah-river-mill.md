# Deep-Audit Dossier — GP Savannah River Mill, Rincon GA (idx 06)

## Resolved location
- **Locked center:** 32.3315, -81.2025
- **Address:** 393 Fort Howard Rd, Rincon, GA 31326 (roster lists 115 Fort Howard Rd; mill office is 393)
- The roster point (32.333323, -81.21165) sat in farmland/woods ~1 km WNW of the mill.
  Probing satellite east from the roster point revealed a large industrial complex.
  Confirmed as the GP Savannah River Mill — a major tissue/towel manufacturing
  campus built in 1986 by Fort Howard Paper, acquired by GP in 2002, ~1,000
  employees, producing toilet paper, paper towels and napkins largely from
  recycled fiber.
