## GP Anchor Packaging Plant — Paragould, AR

**Type:** Thermoformed / Cling Film Packaging Plant
**Resolved coordinates:** 36.08400, -90.46650 (building centroid)
**Confidence:** High

### Location resolution
The roster's geocoded point (36.052562, -90.48868, "APPROXIMATE") landed in
downtown Paragould, ~4 km SW of the actual plant — wrong. Web research
identified Anchor Packaging's Paragould manufacturing plant at **2211 N 12th
Ave** (a separate distribution office exists at 1600 Jones Rd). Geocoding the
street address put the building near 36.0857,-90.4671. Satellite probes
confirmed a large (~310,000+ sq ft, expanded with a 90,000 sq ft addition in
2022) sheet-extrusion/thermoforming plant on the NE edge of Paragould.
Building centroid locked at 36.0840,-90.4665.

### Key views
- **Wide z16/z17:** Single dominant manufacturing building with a separate
  large warehouse immediately NE; large striped employee parking lots on the
  NW side; access road looping in from the SW. Surrounded by farmland and
  scattered light industry — Rural setting.
- **Entrance (Street View 2025-05 + 2023-08):** The private access driveway
  meets the public road with NO barrier arm, NO sliding/swing gate, and NO
  guard booth. Only a "Speed Limit 15" sign on the drive. The site has no
  perimeter fence along the road frontage — wide open grass fields between
  road and building.
- **Dock face (z19/z20):** Dock doors with ~9 trailers backed in along the
  SW face of the building; a paved/graded dock apron in front. Estimated
  ~16 dock doors.
- **East side:** A rail line runs NE-SW ~120 m east of the plant but is
  separated by a wooded buffer — no spur into the property. Not rail-served.

### Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **truckGate: false** — Open private driveway from the public road; no

  captures (2023 and 2025).
- **guardShack: false** — No staffed booth at the entrance or along the

- **remoteGs: false** — No truck gate exists, so no remote check-in either.
- **dockDoors: 10-25** — ~16 doors on the SW face; low-confidence count.
- **dropArea: 0-10** — Trailers stand on the dock apron and a graded dirt

### Web findings
Anchor Packaging's Paragould plant does sheet extrusion and thermoforming of
rigid plastic foodservice containers and cling films; reached ~310,000 sq ft
after a $21.5M / 90,000 sq ft expansion completed in 2022. Acquired by
Georgia-Pacific (acquisition completed Oct 2025). BRC Packaging "High
Hygiene" certified.

### Final confidence
**High** — facility positively identified, imagery clear, gate/guard-shack
calls firm from two Street View dates. Dock-door count and trailer-parking
capacity are honest overhead estimates and flagged uncertain.
