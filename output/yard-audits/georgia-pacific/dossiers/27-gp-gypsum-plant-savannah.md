# GP Gypsum Plant — Savannah, GA

## Resolved location
**Roster coordinates were wrong.** The roster gave 2861 Tremont Rd
(32.058166, -81.13432), which lands in a mixed light-industrial /
scrap-yard area roughly 2.5 km southwest of the river — no gypsum
wallboard plant there.

Web research (Georgia EPD Title V permit narrative, Coastal GA DNR
outfall notice, multiple business directories) established the real
plant address as **151 Wahlstrom Road, Savannah, GA 31404**, on the
south bank of the Savannah River about 1.13 miles downstream of the
Talmadge Bridge. Locked center: **32.0770, -81.0525** — a large
riverfront gypsum complex, formerly Domtar Gypsum, acquired by GP in
1996.

## Web findings
GP Gypsum Savannah, 151 Wahlstrom Rd, formerly Domtar Gypsum (GP
acquired 1996). Produces gypsum board, DensGlass, ToughRock, DensArmor.
Processes mined gypsum rock delivered by ship plus synthetic/FGD
gypsum. 2008 $100M upgrade added a new wallboard line and a
state-of-the-art loading system. Redistributes product by vessel,
truck and rail.
