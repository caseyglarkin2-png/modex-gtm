## Resolved location
- **Coordinates:** 37.5283, -79.3540 (mill core)
- **Address:** 10097 / 9909 Lee Jackson Hwy (US-501), Big Island, VA 24526
- **Type:** Containerboard pulp/paper mill (Georgia-Pacific Packaging & Cellulose)
- Roster coordinates (37.52707, -79.35375) landed inside the complex; confirmed and refined via satellite. The mill is a ~1 km-long integrated facility on the **west bank of the James River**, east of the Norfolk Southern rail line and US-501.
- Web research corroborates: Big Island Paper Mill is **Virginia's oldest continuously operating business** (125+ years), a containerboard mill, EPA Energy Star certified 2024. CLUI and Forest Products Locator both list it as the Georgia-Pacific Big Island Mill.
