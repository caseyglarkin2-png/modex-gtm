# GP Gypsum Plant — Sweetwater, TX

## Resolved location
**Roster coordinates were wrong.** The roster gave 32.470952,
-100.405938 (geocode flagged APPROXIMATE, moved 4088 m), which lands in
downtown Sweetwater, TX — no gypsum plant there.

Web research established the GP Gypsum complex sits on **FM 1856 off
I-20, east of Sweetwater** in Nolan County. The legacy plant (310 FM
1856, operating since 1957) and the new **$325M wallboard plant (311 FM
1856, opened October 2023)** are adjacent on the same campus. Locked
center: **32.4830, -100.3345** — confirmed by GP-branded blue
privacy-screen fencing along the perimeter in Street View.

## Web findings
GP cut the ribbon on the new $325M Sweetwater wallboard plant on
Oct 4, 2023 — GP's second gypsum plant in Nolan County and first new
build since 2004. The complex is >700,000 sq ft; combined output >1
billion sq ft of gypsum products/year; 100+ new jobs. FM 1856 was
closed for construction to add water/sewer and a new rail crossing.
