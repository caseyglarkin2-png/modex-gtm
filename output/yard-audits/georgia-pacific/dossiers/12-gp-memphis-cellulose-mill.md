# Deep-Audit Dossier — idx 12: GP Memphis Cellulose Mill, Memphis TN

## Resolved location
- **Roster point:** 35.148581, -90.051896 ("100 N B St") — geocoded APPROXIMATE, flagged 1952 m moved. It landed in **downtown Memphis** — clearly wrong.
- **Resolution:** Web research (Greater Memphis Chamber, Waze, GP closure announcements, Tennessee Encyclopedia) established:
  - The **cellulose mill** is at **920 Scott Road**, in the **Binghampton** neighborhood.
  - The **1001 Tillman St** address is a *separate* facility — the GP Cellulose Technology & Innovation Center (an office building, ~22 staff).
  - The site is the former **Buckeye Cellulose / Buckeye Technologies** cotton-linter pulp plant, acquired by GP in 2013.
- Satellite probing located the large fenced multi-building mill complex at ~**35.1565, -89.9620**, bounded by a rail corridor (west) and city streets/residential (east, south).
- Street View (captured 2025-03) along several street frontages confirmed: chain-link perimeter fencing, large processing/warehouse buildings, a rail/tank-car yard, pole-barn storage, and the brick office building — consistent with an aging urban pulp/cellulose mill.

## Key views
- **z16 overview:** Multi-building campus, ~78 acres, rail line the length of the west side, residential streets bordering east and south.
- **z17/z18 core:** Large warehouse/processing buildings (some weathered roofs), employee parking lots, processing tanks and structures, trailers parked near the rail yard.
- **z19 rail yard:** Wide paved yard with rail tank cars, rail equipment and trailers — a drop/storage yard.
- **Street View:** Fenced perimeter confirmed on Tillman St (east), the west rail-side road, and the south road; office/admin building and fenced parking-lot gates at the main entry.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — TRUE.** The complex is fully enclosed by chain-link perimeter fencing on every frontage observed. Controlled vehicle gates are present at the fenced parking/office entry. Classified as a controlled truck gate; a dedicated truck-only lane could not be isolated in imagery — flagged uncertain.
- **Guard shack — FALSE / remoteGs TRUE.** No distinct standalone guard booth confirmed at a truck lane; the brick office/admin building sits at the main entrance and performs the access-control role.
- **Docks — 10-25 band.** Multiple dock banks across the aging buildings; ~8 trailers visible plus additional doors. shipRcvSeparate TRUE — distinct dock clusters on different building faces, plus rail-served receiving.
- **backupSensitive — TRUE.** Urban site fronting directly onto city streets with residential blocks across; a gate queue would spill onto a public street.
- **Scale — TRUE (low confidence).** A truck scale is operationally expected at a pulp mill of this type/era; not pinpointed in imagery.

## Yard zones and counts

- **truckGate zone:** main entry near the office, east side.
- **dropYard:** large paved rail-side yard holding trailers and rail cars.
- **dockApron:** strips in front of the warehouse dock banks.
- **postGateStaging:** ample paved internal yard between gate and docks.
- **Building count:** ~14 distinct structures (processing, warehouses, pole barns, office).
- **Rail-served:** TRUE — active rail line and on-site rail yard.
- **Driveway:** long internal approach (3+ truck capacity).

## Web findings
- GP Memphis Cellulose mill (920 Scott Rd) — cotton-linter pulp / specialty cellulose. Former Buckeye Cellulose, originally a Procter & Gamble facility; bought by an investor group (1992), became Buckeye Technologies; GP acquired Buckeye in 2013.
- **Georgia-Pacific announced in October 2025 it will permanently close the Memphis Cellulose mill** and the Tillman St Technology & Innovation Center, citing challenging market conditions — ~130 layoffs at the mill, ~22 at the T&I Center.

## Final confidence
**Medium.** Facility positively identified and layout characterized from clear satellite + Street View. Confidence held to medium: the roster coordinate was badly off (downtown Memphis) and required full relocation; the dedicated truck gate / guard-shack distinction and truck scale could not be fully resolved from imagery; dock count is a banded estimate. Note also that the mill is slated for permanent closure (Oct 2025 announcement) — yard operations may be winding down.
