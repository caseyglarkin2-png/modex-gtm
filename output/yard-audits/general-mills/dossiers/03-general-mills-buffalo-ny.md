## Location confirmation

The roster geocode (ROOFTOP) landed accurately on the General Mills mill
building. Satellite at zoom 16-19 shows the historic General Mills Cheerios
plant on the narrow spit of land between the Buffalo River and the City Ship
Canal — grain elevators, a 10-floor high-rise manufacturing building, conveyor
bridges, and processing structures. 2025 Street View on South Michigan Avenue
shows GM-branded trailers backed into the street-level docks, confirming
identity. Web research (Buffalo News, Born Buffalo) confirms this plant has
made Cheerios since 1941 and produces ~28% of the world's Cheerios; it also
makes Wheaties, Lucky Charms, Chex and Gold Medal flour.
