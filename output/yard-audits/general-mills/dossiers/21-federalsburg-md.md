## Location confirmation
Satellite probes at z16-z20 around the roster point show a multi-building
industrial complex set in a mixed residential/woodland area on the west bank of
Marshyhope Creek. Web research (Maryland MDE Brownfield VCP record) confirms the
General Mills Operations / former Pillsbury Company property at 300 Reliance
Ave, 21.31 acres, "bounded to the north and west by residences, east by a
commercial business park, south by a wastewater treatment plant" — an exact
match to the imagery. Note: General Mills divested this plant to Kraft Foods in
April 2010 (bread-crumb / Stove Top stuffing production). Street View (2023-07)
shows the gate banner now reads "Eastern Shore Forest Products — Agricultural
Processing Facility," and wood/mulch material piles appear in the SE yard. The
physical plant at the roster address was audited as instructed.
