## Location confirmation
Satellite probes at z16-z21 around the roster point show an industrial park on
the NE edge of Vinita near I-44 / Highway-2. The large white building with a
dark-roof NW section, employee parking, and a big SW paved lot matches the
General Mills frozen-dough plant. Web research (Showcase.com / LoopNet listing
and Yelp/D&B) confirms 1157 Doughboy Drive: ~110,000 sq ft, ~9.7 acres, built
1995, 8 dock doors, 5 spiral freezers, 13,800 sq ft fully-racked freezer,
ammonia refrigeration — one of the four ex-Pillsbury frozen-dough plants
General Mills acquired in 2001. The plant is currently marketed for sale.
