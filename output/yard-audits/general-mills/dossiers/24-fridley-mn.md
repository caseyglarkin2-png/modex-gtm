## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (45.048708, -93.270153, GEOMETRIC_CENTER, moved 2232 m)
landed on the east edge of the industrial site. Satellite probing at z16-z18
revealed the actual mill complex slightly west-southwest: a multi-story
concrete mill tower, a cluster of large concrete grain-elevator silos, a long
row of low metal grain-storage silos along the rail yard, and conveyor
bridges. Web research confirmed this is the General Mills Fridley plant
("Mill O"), an oat-milling facility that cleans and mills oats for Cheerios
and Lucky Charms (address 25 44th Ave NE; World Grain / industry directories).
Center locked at 45.04830, -93.27080.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — FALSE.** The truck entrance is a single wide, open paved
  driveway off the public access road at the SE corner of the property.
  Street View (2022-06 and 2021-08) from the public road shows no barrier
  arm, no slide/swing gate, and no checkpoint at the property line — the
  driveway simply opens onto the road.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No guard booth at the entrance in any Street View
  heading. A small structure near the mill building is a utility/equipment
  structure, not a gate booth.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** No gate, so no remote check-in.
- **Dock doors — 0-10 band.** As a flour mill, the facility ships and
  receives primarily by rail and bulk tanker truck rather than palletized
  dock-door freight. Only a modest set of loading positions (~6 estimated)
  along the building faces; no large dock-door bank. Low confidence.

## Web findings
General Mills Fridley plant, known internally as "Mill O" in the Operations
Division Milling. The plant cleans and mills oats made into General Mills
cereals, most notably Cheerios and Lucky Charms. Address 25 44th Ave NE,
Fridley MN 55421. Listed in World Grain and other grain-milling directories.

## Final confidence

confirmed from multiple Street View captures; rail service unambiguous from
satellite. Dock-door count and trailer capacity are honest estimates from
