# Deep-Audit Dossier — General Mills, Cedar Rapids IA (Edgewood Rd)

**Roster idx:** 2
**Facility type:** Manufacturing Plant (cereal / food)
**Resolved address:** 4800 Edgewood Rd SW, Cedar Rapids, IA 52404
**Locked center:** 41.93220, -91.72020
**Confidence:** High

## Location confirmation

The roster geocode (41.933068, -91.719609, ROOFTOP) landed accurately on the
General Mills process building. Satellite at zoom 15-18 shows a large food
manufacturing complex with dense rooftop process equipment, a rail-served
loading area, a large SW distribution warehouse, and a major trailer drop yard
to the NW — fully consistent with a cereal plant. The Cedar Rapids Metro
Economic Alliance directory lists this as the single General Mills Operations
location in Cedar Rapids (member since 1970). Street View along the access road
off Edgewood Rd SW shows General Mills signage ("Frontage Road" / "South
Entrance" directional sign) confirming identity.

Note: roster idx 1 ("Wenig Rd NE") refers to the same company in the same city;
that address is erroneous (it geocodes to the Cedar Rapids Water Pollution
Control Facility). idx 1 has been audited as this same physical plant.

## Key views

- **Wide (z15-16):** Central process complex, SW warehouse, NW drop yard,
  employee parking, and an extensive multi-track rail yard along the south edge.
  Separate (non-GM) white warehouses sit NW and NE of the property.
- **Tight process complex (z19-20):** Dense interconnected manufacturing
  buildings with heavy rooftop process equipment — characteristic cereal plant.
- **SW warehouse (z18-19):** Continuous dock bank on the SE face with ~20+
  trailers backed in.
- **Rail yard (z19):** Many covered hopper cars on multiple tracks; rail spurs
  enter rail-served loading canopies — definitively rail-served.
- **NW drop yard (z18):** Organized trailer storage lot with ~120-150 trailers
  in marked rows.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate:** TRUE. The property is fully enclosed by a black ornamental
  metal perimeter fence (clearly visible in 2019 and 2024 Street View). A single
  private access road serves the campus from Edgewood Rd SW. Controlled truck
  entrance.
- **Guard shack:** FALSE / uncertain. No booth visible — Street View panos stop
  at the public road and do not extend onto the private entry drive. A booth
  could exist beyond the fence but none was seen.
- **Remote GS:** TRUE (assumed) — gate present, no confirmed staffed booth.
  Low confidence, flagged.
- **Dock doors:** 50+ band. SW warehouse dock bank plus central process and
  rail-served docks.
- **Ship/Rcv separate:** TRUE — docks on physically separate building faces.

## Web findings

- Cedar Rapids Metro Economic Alliance and multiple business directories
  confirm 4800 Edgewood Rd SW as the General Mills Operations plant; it is the
  only GM facility in Cedar Rapids. A $37M expansion of the Cedar Rapids plant
  has been reported in local news (KHAK).

## Final confidence

High on location, layout, rail service, dock band, and drop-yard scale.
Guard-shack and lane counts flagged uncertain because Street View does not
reach the gate.
