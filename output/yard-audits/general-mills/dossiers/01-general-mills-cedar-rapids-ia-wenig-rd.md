## Roster error — important

The roster lists idx 1 at "1000 Wenig Rd NE" with a ROOFTOP geocode. Satellite
imagery of that point (41.998837, -91.676411) at zoom 15-17 shows the **Cedar
Rapids Water Pollution Control Facility** — a wastewater treatment plant with
unmistakable circular clarifier / aeration tanks. There is no General Mills
plant at that address.

Web research (Cedar Rapids Metro Economic Alliance industrial directory)
confirms there is **only one** General Mills facility in Cedar Rapids: the
cereal manufacturing plant at **4800 Edgewood Rd SW** — which is roster idx 2.

**Conclusion:** roster idx 1 and idx 2 are the same physical facility. The
Wenig Rd address was a bad entry. This dossier audits the real General Mills
Cedar Rapids plant; the classification is identical to idx 2.
