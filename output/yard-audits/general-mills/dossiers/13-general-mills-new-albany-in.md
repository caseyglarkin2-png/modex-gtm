# General Mills - New Albany IN (idx 13)

## Resolved location
- Roster coords (38.328544, -85.82367, geocode precision GEOMETRIC_CENTER) **landed on the correct site** — the large industrial complex at **707 Pillsbury Lane, New Albany IN**. Locked center **38.3284, -85.8240**.
- **Ownership note:** This is the **former General Mills Pillsbury dough/biscuit plant** (founded 1959, ~460,000 sq ft). General Mills **closed it in 2016** and sold it (via New Mill Capital / Tiger Group) to **The Sazerac Company in June 2018**. It now operates as **Northwest Ordinance Distilling** (Sazerac) — a spirits processing, blending, bottling and distribution facility with 200+ employees. The roster's General Mills attribution (Hyster GM plant list) is stale. The site remains an active manufacturing/distribution freight facility and is audited as it physically stands today.
