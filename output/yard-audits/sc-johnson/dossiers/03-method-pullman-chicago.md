## Web findings
The method South Side Soapbox is a 150,000 sq ft factory, the first LEED
Platinum plant in the cleaners industry, designed by William McDonough +
Partners on cradle-to-cradle principles. It manufactures and bottles method
and Ecover products and functions as their distribution point. The site
features a 230-ft wind turbine and solar "trees" supplying over a third of
the building's electricity, with ~18.5 of its 22 acres returned to prairie.
Gotham Greens runs a 75,000 sq ft rooftop greenhouse atop the plant.
