# Deep-Audit Dossier — Crowley JAXPORT Marine Terminal (idx 1)

**Facility:** Crowley JAXPORT Marine Terminal — Jacksonville, FL
**Type:** Marine terminal / container port (ConRo — Puerto Rico / Caribbean)
**Roster address:** 2831 Talleyrand Avenue, Jacksonville, FL 32206
**Audited center:** 30.354400, -81.623900

## Step 0 — Site identification

The roster point (30.356209, -81.625015 / 2831 Talleyrand Ave) is the
**Crowley Main Port Office** — an L-shaped office building ringed by employee
parking, confirmed at satellite z19. The office is not the operational yard;
the audited facility is the **Crowley Talleyrand container terminal** that the
office serves. Wide imagery (z15) shows a continuous container terminal running
roughly 1 km along the west bank of the St. Johns River: container stacks,
chassis/trailer yards, gantry-crane berths, transit-shed warehouses, and a tank
farm at the north end. Web research (crowley.com, jaxport.com) confirms Crowley
runs LNG-powered ConRo ships from JAXPORT's Talleyrand Marine Terminal and that
the **north gate** was reconstructed during a terminal modernization.

Audited center set to the terminal interior (~30.3544, -81.6239).

## Gate / guard / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — TRUE.** A purpose-built, modernized covered gate complex
  (the "north gate" Crowley reconstructed). Multi-lane canopy with painted lane
  chevrons; clearly a controlled checkpoint between Talleyrand Avenue and the
  terminal.
- **Guard shack — TRUE.** Check-in booth modules are built into / beside the
  gate canopy, plus a separate small booth structure near the road. Staffed
  gate operation (port terminals require TWIC-controlled access).
- **remoteGs — FALSE** (guard shack present).
- **Lanes:** ~3 inbound, ~2 outbound under the canopy (approximate).
- **fastLaneOpportunity — TRUE.** Wide apron and multi-lane canopy give room
  for an express/bypass lane.
- **Docks:** As a marine terminal the cargo interface is crane-served berths,
  not a warehouse dock bank. Loading doors exist on the riverside transit shed
  (south end) — counted ~12, banded "10-25", low confidence.

## Yard zones & counts

- **Perimeter:** ~197 acres capturing the Crowley terminal from the north gate
  south to the transit-shed/berth area.
- **Drop yards:** Three large container/chassis stacking areas mapped; hundreds
  of trailers/containers visible.
- **Staging:** Apron outside/around the gate canopy serves as pre-gate staging.
- **Rail-served:** TRUE — rail spurs enter the terminal NW of the gate and
  along the west edge; Talleyrand also has an adjacent intermodal yard (ICTF).
- **Buildings:** ~9 distinct structures (office, transit sheds, gate buildings,
  tank-farm structures) — campus-scale, multipleFacilities TRUE.

## Web findings

crowley.com / jaxport.com confirm: Crowley operates LNG ConRo service to San
Juan from Talleyrand; the terminal was modernized with a reconstructed north
gate, repaved/restriped yard, reducing trucker turn times to ~20 minutes.

## Confidence

**High.** Facility positively identified; gate, guard, drop yard, rail all
confirmed with imagery. Dock-door band and presence of a truck scale are
low-confidence (flagged in uncertainFields).
