## Location resolution
Roster coordinates (39.853752, -75.338927) landed inside the terminal —
confirmed correct (ROOFTOP geocode). Penn Terminals sits at the end of Saville
Avenue on the Delaware River in Eddystone, PA. Web research confirmed the
address and that the terminal is "straight ahead at the end of Saville Avenue."
Satellite imagery shows the long dry warehouse, refrigerated warehouse, a large
container/chassis storage yard, and a river pier. Street View along Saville Ave
shows the terminal warehouse, chain-link perimeter fence, and the historic
brick Penn Terminals office building near the entrance.
