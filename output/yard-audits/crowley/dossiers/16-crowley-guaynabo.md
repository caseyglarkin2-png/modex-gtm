# Crowley Liner & Logistics — Guaynabo (Amelia / FTZ 61)

**Resolved location:** Building #13, Free Trade Zone #61, Carretera 165 (Rd 165) KM 2.4, Guaynabo, PR 00965.
Locked center: **18.42462, -66.12080**.
[Satellite](https://www.google.com/maps/@18.42462,-66.12080,400m/data=!3m1!1e3)

## What it is
Crowley's consolidated **NVO/LCL + warehouse / distribution center** for Puerto Rico (Crowley's "Guaynabo warehouse location change" announcement: NVO/LCL ops merged with warehouse & distribution into one Edificio-13 site with expanded yard, more receiving doors, and dock space). This is the **liner-logistics warehouse**, distinct from:
- Crowley's **waterfront container/chassis marshalling yard** (~18.4259, -66.1119, ~1 km NE on the Puerto Nuevo bay) — acres of blue Crowley trailers/containers, vessel-side.
- The **Isla Grande ConRo terminal** (separate audit, site 06) — the marine terminal proper.

## How the location was confirmed
1. Web research resolved the address to Edificio 13 / FTZ 61 / Carretera 165 KM 2.4, Guaynabo; the address geocodes to the **Amelia Industrial Park**.
2. Two candidate areas were probed by satellite (the Amelia warehouse cluster and the waterfront blue-trailer yard).
3. **Decisive evidence — Street View:** the internal park-road pano at 18.42414, -66.12042 (captured 2023-09, looking W) shows a blue building with **"CROWLEY"** painted on the wall behind the south fence. The east-end pano at 18.42415, -66.11983 shows **CROWLEY-branded** red containers plus **TOTE** and blue containers staged on the dock apron. That positively pins the long E-W gray-roof cross-dock building as the Crowley facility.
