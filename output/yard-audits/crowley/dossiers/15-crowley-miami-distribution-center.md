# Crowley Miami Distribution Center - Medley FL

## Location resolution & confirmation

Web research resolved the Crowley Miami DC to **10205 NW 108th Ave, Medley FL 33178**
(Crowley press release: the Medley warehouse consolidated Crowley's former Medley
and Doral facilities, expanded to ~180,000 sq ft, with double the prior receiving
doors, 5,000+ pallet rack positions, and **95 container parking spots**).

Google rooftop geocode of the address returned **25.8656091, -80.37252** — landing
on a long warehouse in the Beacon Lakes / Doral-Medley industrial park, oriented
NNW-SSE, with its dock face and a large container yard on the **east** side and an
office/employee-parking frontage on the **west**.

**Positive identification (Street View, 2025-10):** at the north truck entrance a
**navy CROWLEY intermodal container** and a **red CROWLEY trailer** are parked in
the yard, with CROWLEY signage on the building. The dock-line yard view (east side,
looking west) shows steamship-line containers backed into the docks, including a
readable **MAERSK** box and additional CROWLEY units — unmistakably Crowley's
marine/intermodal LCL operation. This is the correct building, not a neighboring
tenant.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Single gated truck entrance at the north drive: a green
  chain-link sliding/rolling gate across the full lane, with perimeter fence and
  jersey barriers. Controlled entry, confirmed in 2025-10 Street View.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed booth at the gate in Street View or zoom-20
  satellite — only the sliding gate, fence, and barriers.
- **Remote gate (remoteGs) — TRUE.** Gate present, no guard shack → access by
  card/kiosk/app at the sliding gate.
- **Dock doors — 50+.** Near-continuous dock line along the ~768 ft east face with
  containers/trailers backed in; estimated **~58 doors** (overhead estimate, flagged).
- **Ship/Rcv separate — FALSE.** All docks on one (east) building face.
- **Scale — FALSE.** No weigh pad in the truck path.
