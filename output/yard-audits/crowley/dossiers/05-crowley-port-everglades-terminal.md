## Step 0 — Site identification

4300 McIntosh Road is the **Crowley Port Everglades Terminal**, a 78-acre
container terminal in the Southport area of Port Everglades. The roster
coordinates are accurate. Satellite imagery (z15-z21) shows a large container
terminal — container stacks, chassis/trailer yards, gantry-crane berths along
the waterway, and intermodal rail spurs on the west side. Web research
(crowley.com, porteverglades.net, petpev.com) confirms the terminal: 78 acres,
8 lanes, 4 scales, 3 berths, 5,000-FEU yard capacity, rail access.
