## Step 0 — Site identification

3001 Talleyrand Avenue is **Crowley Port Office #3**. The roster point lands
essentially on the **modernized north-gate canopy** of the Talleyrand terminal.
This is the same physical terminal as idx 1 (2831 Talleyrand, Main Port Office)
and idx 4 (2085 Talleyrand) — three Crowley offices on one terminal complex.
Idx 3 is audited as the **northern terminal section centered on the north
gate**. Web research (crowley.com) confirms Office #3 handles containers and
individual vehicle shippers to Puerto Rico and Central America.
