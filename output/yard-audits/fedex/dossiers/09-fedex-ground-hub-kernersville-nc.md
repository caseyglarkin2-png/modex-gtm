# FedEx Ground Hub - Kernersville NC (idx 9)

## Resolved location
- **Audited center:** 36.10625, -80.03550 — FedEx Ground regional sortation hub, Triad
  Business Park, Kernersville/Guilford County NC.
- **Roster coordinate** (36.106996, -80.035813) landed directly on the hub building. The
  geocode moved 9.1 km from the listed address (1544 Old Greensboro Rd) but the resulting
  coordinate is correct — Step 0 satellite immediately showed an unmistakable FedEx Ground hub
  (central sort building ringed by hundreds of trailer-parking rows).
- Verified by web research: FedEx Ground opened this hub in October 2011 at 410,000 sq ft and
  expanded it in 2015 to ~493,000 sq ft, on a ~125-acre site in Triad Business Park.
