# Deep-Audit Dossier — FedEx Express World Hub, Memphis TN (idx 01)

## Resolved location
- **Coordinates:** 35.061887, -89.960429 (roster point, on the main sort building)
- **Address:** 2903 Sprankle Ave, Memphis, TN 38118
- **Confirmed:** Roster point lands squarely on the FedEx Express World Hub ("Superhub"), the company's largest sort facility, on the west side of Memphis International Airport. Satellite imagery shows multiple massive sort buildings, 100+ freighter aircraft parking positions (numbered airside gates 209/210/259/260/679...), and acres of ULD/air-container staging. Web research corroborates: FedEx newsroom describes it as the largest sort facility in the global network — ~940 acres, 171 aircraft gates, 84 miles of conveyor, 13,000 team members, with a new 1.3M sq ft automated sort facility added in 2024.

- **Wide (z15):** Whole campus between airport runways and the surrounding road grid — a contiguous industrial complex of sort buildings, hangars, support buildings, and enormous paved aprons.
- **z16/z17 overview:** Main sort building plus the new automated sort facility; landside support area with very large employee parking lots and FedEx delivery-vehicle staging rows.
- **z18-z20 sort building:** Airside ramp with numbered aircraft positions and ULD staging — primary cargo transfer is aircraft-side, not truck-dock-side.
- **z19 north of sort buildings:** Landside road-feeder vehicle operations — rows of straight trucks and trailers staged between buildings, with an internal pinch-point/checkpoint structure.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Hardened secured campus. Street View along the perimeter shows steel anti-ram palisade fencing with outriggers, chain-link topped with coiled razor wire and privacy screening, Jersey-barrier lane channelization on internal roads, and a canopied multi-lane vehicle checkpoint. Web research confirms a dedicated FedEx Main Screening Facility (Flintco-built, 44,000 SF) as the controlled main point of entry.
- **Guard shack — TRUE.** The Main Screening Facility is a staffed 44,000 SF checkpoint building — far more than a booth, but functionally the staffed gate control. Multiple manned checkpoints serve the ~5-mile perimeter.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** Entry is staffed/screened, not kiosk-only.
- **Multi-step — TRUE.** Perimeter vehicle screening checkpoint, then secondary controlled access into airside operations.
- **Docks — 50+.** Air-to-ground hub: primary transfer is aircraft-side. Landside truck docks for road feeder vehicles are dispersed across sort buildings; door count well into the 50+ band (estimate from imagery).
- **Drop area — 50+.** Extensive road-feeder vehicle and trailer staging visible between sort buildings and in landside lots.
