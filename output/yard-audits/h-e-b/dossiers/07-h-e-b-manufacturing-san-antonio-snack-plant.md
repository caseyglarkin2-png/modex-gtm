## Location confirmation
The roster supplied only "San Antonio, TX" and an APPROXIMATE coordinate that had
moved 6794 m — it was unusable. Web research positively located the plant:
- Multiple sources (San Antonio Express-News / Yahoo, TDLR TABS, Zabalist, Waze,
  city-data) place the H-E-B Snack Plant at **5311 Rittiman Rd, San Antonio, TX
  78218**, on the city's far Northeast Side, "near the company's refrigerated
  distribution center and fleet site."
- A 2025 TDLR renovation project ("H.E.B. SAS Bakery Chips," 8,000 sq ft, $13.0M)
  describes alterations inside the existing food-manufacturing plant to accept a
  new production line.
- Satellite and Street View confirm the H-E-B Rittiman/Fratt Rd manufacturing &
  distribution campus: a large RSC warehouse with rooftop solar, fleet operations,
  and many H-E-B red-branded trailers in campus drop yards.

The campus is positively the correct location. The specific snack-plant building
within the campus could not be confirmed by visible signage; the audited building
(~29.4850, -98.3893) is the strongest manufacturing-plant candidate — a square
food-processing building with extensive rooftop process/HVAC equipment and a small
annex. Building-level pin is medium confidence.

## Web findings
H-E-B's first snack manufacturing facility — ~40,000 sq ft, produces private-label
potato, corn and tortilla chips, operates 24/7, processing 35M lb of potatoes and
10M lb of corn annually. Part of H-E-B's manufacturing concentration on San
Antonio's NE/East Side (milk plant at 4710 N I-35, warehouse at 5003 Eisenhauer
Rd). H-E-B is master-planning the campus for additional manufacturing, warehouse
and transportation use.
