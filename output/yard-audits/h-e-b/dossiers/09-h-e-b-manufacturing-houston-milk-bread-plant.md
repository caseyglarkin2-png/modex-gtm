## Location confirmation
The roster supplied a GEOMETRIC_CENTER coordinate (29.843857, -95.537401) that is
roughly the centroid of the H-E-B Windfern campus parcel; it landed near campus
internal roads. Web research confirms H-E-B operates a milk plant and a bakery/
bread plant at 4625 Windfern Rd — the same address as the Windfern RSC; all
H-E-B Windfern facilities share that campus and address.

buildings immediately east of the snack plant on the campus. The eastern building
is positively confirmed as the bakery — "BAKE..." (Bakery) branded H-E-B trailers
are backed into its west dock face. The western building, with a process annex,
is the milk plant. Exact milk-vs-bread building assignment is medium confidence.
