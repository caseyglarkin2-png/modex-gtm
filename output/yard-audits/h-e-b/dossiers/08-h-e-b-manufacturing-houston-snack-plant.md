# Deep-Audit Dossier — H-E-B Manufacturing, Houston Snack Plant (Genard Road)

**Facility:** H-E-B Manufacturing - Houston Snack Plant (Manufacturing Plant, Snacks / Tortilla Chips)
**Address:** 10000 Genard Rd, Houston, TX 77041
**Resolved center:** 29.843800, -95.541300
**Confidence:** Medium

## Location confirmation
The roster's ROOFTOP geocode (moved only 4 m) landed correctly. Satellite imagery
at z18-z20 shows a manufacturing building with heavy rooftop process equipment and
white circular corn/grain silos — exactly consistent with a tortilla-chip plant.
Web research confirms: H-E-B opened a ~46,000 sq ft (some sources 64,000 sq ft)
snack manufacturing plant at 10000 ½ Genard Rd in mid-2019, producing plain and
flavored tortilla chips, "in the same complex as the retailer's milk plant, bread
facility and distribution center" — i.e. the H-E-B NW Houston campus. Building
identity is positively confirmed.

## Key views
- **Wide campus (z16):** Very large multi-building H-E-B campus — RSC warehouses,
  milk plant, bread plant, the snack plant, and extensive trailer drop yards.
- **Snack plant (z18/z20 @ ~29.8438, -95.5413):** L-shaped building with heavy
  rooftop process/HVAC equipment and corn silos; employee parking to the north;
  dock face on the NE/E side with trailers backed in.
- **Drop yards (z18/z20):** A large trailer drop yard immediately south of the
  plant holds dozens of trailers; additional trailer parking to the NE.
- **Campus perimeter (Street View):** Decorative metal perimeter fencing along the
  campus; entrances probed appear as open paved driveways.

## Gate / guard-shack determination
- **truckGate = false.** The snack plant sits inside the large fenced H-E-B Houston
  campus. The campus has decorative metal perimeter fencing, but no barrier arm,
  sliding gate or controlled checkpoint was positively identified at the campus
  entrances in satellite or Street View — the entrances probed are open paved
  driveways. Flagged uncertain: a controlled campus gate could exist beyond the
  probed entrances.
- **guardShack = false.** No guard booth identified at the snack-plant area or the
  campus entrances probed.
- **remoteGs = false** (no controlled gate established).

## Web findings
H-E-B Houston snack plant — ~46,000 sq ft (later cited at 64,000 sq ft), opened
mid-2019 at 10000 ½ Genard Rd, produces plain and flavored tortilla chips, built
to FSMA standards with autonomous-operation capability. Operates less than a mile
from the H-E-B distribution center at 4625 Windfern Rd and shares the campus with
the milk and bread plants.

## Final confidence
Medium. Building identity and campus context are positively confirmed, but the
gate/guard-shack determination relies on the campus entrances visible in imagery —
no controlled checkpoint was positively located, though the campus is fenced.
