## Web findings
The St. Louis brewery (founded 1852) is Anheuser-Busch's flagship and the
largest single-site brewery in the world, employing on-site rail heavily and
operating a major distribution warehouse alongside the brewing and packaging
operations. The campus is a designated National Historic Landmark district.
