# Mondelez / Nabisco Tatamy Sales Distribution Center — Tatamy, PA

**Facility type:** Distribution center / sales warehouse
**Resolved coordinates:** 40.73510, -75.26120 (120 Commerce Lane, Tatamy PA 18085)
**Confidence:** High

## Location resolution

The roster supplied city-centroid coordinates (40.7401, -75.2557) with no street
address — ~1 km northeast of the actual facility. Web research resolved the
address: Becknell Industrial built a ~100,300 sq ft build-to-suit Nabisco /
Mondelez food-and-beverage distribution warehouse in Tatamy, and the Becknell
property listing, a Foursquare DC listing, and the Mondelez hourly-jobs portal
all give the branch address as **120 Commerce Lane, Tatamy PA 18085**
(Northampton County, Lehigh Valley; the Becknell page also shows the
Palmer-Township ZIP 18045).

A Nominatim geocode (40.7362, -75.2619) put the search in the Becknell
industrial park; satellite then identified the modern grey warehouse with purple
accent striping — a build-to-suit DC with a fenced truck/drop yard on its south
face, matching the documented ~100,300 sq ft Nabisco facility.
