**Facility type:** Manufacturing / warehouse — cookie bakery + distribution
**Resolved coordinates:** 40.84720, -72.87170 (111 Precision Drive, Shirley NY 11967)
**Confidence:** High

## Location resolution

The roster supplied city-centroid coordinates (40.8015, -72.8743) with no
street address. Web research established that in December 2023 Mondelez moved
Tate's into a newly-constructed warehouse and distribution development in
Shirley; the **Precision Innovation Park** — a two-building, ~400,000 sq ft
Class-A logistics park delivered 2023 directly off the Long Island Expressway
(I-495) in the Brookhaven research/technology park — is the matching
development.

Positive confirmation came from Street View (June 2024): a monument sign at the
park entrance reads **"TATE'S — PICK UP AND DELIVERIES — 111 Precision Drive —
ENTRANCE."** The corrected coordinates point to the large Class-A warehouse on
the south side of the LIE service road. (Notably, the roster's supplied
longitude, -72.8743, already matched Precision Drive almost exactly — only the
latitude was off.)
