# Mondelez Toledo Flour Mill — Toledo, OH

**Facility type:** Manufacturing — flour mill (input / ingredient)

## Location resolution

The roster supplied city-centroid coordinates (41.6528, -83.5379) with no street
address — these fell ~2 km southwest in a downtown-Toledo riverfront park.
Research established the published address as **2221 Front Street, Toledo OH
43605** (Mennel Milling site, OSHA inspection record, World-Grain directory).
The mill sits in the historic **Birmingham / Ironville** heavy-industrial
district of East Toledo, on the east bank of the Maumee River. A Nominatim
geocode of the street address (41.6640, -83.4978) put the search in the right
neighborhood; satellite then revealed the grain-elevator + milling complex, and
Street View (Aug 2023) gave positive confirmation — the riverside silo cluster
is branded **"Mondelez."**

**Ownership note:** Mennel Milling completed acquisition of this mill from
Mondelez International in **November 2025**. It was a Mondelez facility for the
campaign period and is now Mennel-operated; the physical yard is unchanged.

## Facility character

This is **not a packaged-goods distribution center** — it is a bulk soft-wheat
flour mill: a ~61,000 sq ft six-story mill (current structure built 1976,
milling on the site since 1892) plus extensive grain-elevator silos with
~4.9-5.5M bushels of storage. It is the largest dedicated soft-wheat mill in
North America and historically supplied >80% of the flour for the Nabisco /
Kraft / Mondelez biscuit network. Truck activity is bulk flour loadout and bulk
wheat receiving via silo spouts; there is no conventional dock-door bank or
trailer drop yard.
