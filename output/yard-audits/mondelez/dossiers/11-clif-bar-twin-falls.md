## Location resolution

The roster supplied city-centroid coordinates (42.5826, -114.4286) which fell in
a residential / farm area roughly 5 km northwest of the actual plant. Web
research (Idaho Commerce ground-breaking release, BoiseDev, Magic Valley /
Times-News, Twin Falls Chamber) places the Clif Bar bakery on a ~90-acre parcel
in the **Jayco Industrial Park**, in the area of **Champlin Road and Eldridge
Avenue** in southeast Twin Falls — street address commonly listed as **3438
Eldridge Ave** (ZIP 83341). The Chobani plant (3450 Kimberly Rd) is the large
1M+ sq ft white facility immediately to the northwest, consistent with the
roster note "near the Chobani plant."

Satellite confirmation: the building is the distinctive ~275,000 sq ft Clif Bar
bakery — an L-shaped plant with a covered dock on its west face, an extensive
landscaped/open campus, and on-site solar arrays (a ground-mount field southwest
of the building and a solar-canopy carport over employee parking), matching the
plant's documented biophilic / sustainability design and its 2016 opening.
Acquired by Mondelez June 2022 in the $2.9B Clif Bar deal; still operating.
