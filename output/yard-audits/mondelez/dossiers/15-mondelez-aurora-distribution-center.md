# Mondelez Aurora Distribution Center (DHL / Exel-operated) — Aurora, IL

**Facility type:** Distribution center (3PL-operated, DHL / Exel Logistics)
**Resolved coordinates:** 41.73550, -88.34700 (2380 Sullivan Rd, Aurora IL 60506)
**Confidence:** High

## Location resolution

The roster supplied coordinates (41.7405, -88.3506) and the street address
2380 Sullivan Rd, Aurora IL 60506. The roster coords were ~0.6 km northwest of
the building. Web research (Waze "DHL Mondelez," LoopNet, businessyab,
Illinois-pages) all corroborate **2380 Sullivan Rd** as the DHL Supply Chain /
Exel Logistics distribution center serving Mondelez / Kraft Foods — a ~533,232
sq ft warehouse (~144,112 sq ft of it temperature-controlled) with immediate
I-88 access. Satellite and Street View confirmed the large warehouse on the
corrected coordinates.

**Operational note:** one real-estate listing flags the building as possibly
available / for lease, while other 2025 sources still reference it as
operational. The current satellite shows a vast but nearly empty truck yard,
consistent with reduced operations or a transitional state.
