## Step 0 - Facility confirmation
Web research resolved the GM "Minneapolis" CCA Parts Distribution Center to GM's
Hudson, WI facility at 2200 Willis Miller Drive (404,000 sq ft, opened fall 2004,
UAW Local 722, fulfills GM dealer and ACDelco service-parts orders). Two candidate
coordinate sets were returned by search; satellite probes at both showed the same
large warehouse - candidate A (44.9455, -92.7017) centered it. A z16-18 sweep
positively identified one large rectangular distribution building running roughly
E-W on the north side of Willis Miller Drive, just south of I-94 and a roundabout,
with an attached office wing at its SE corner. This matches a ~404k sq ft single-
building PDC, not an office. Building locked; everything below uses it.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate: FALSE.** 2024 Street View from several headings along Willis
  Miller Dr shows an open, deep landscaped lawn frontage with NO perimeter fence,
  NO barrier arm, and NO checkpoint pinch-point at the truck driveway. Trucks roll
  straight off the public road onto the dock apron. Open corporate-campus PDC.
- **Guard shack: FALSE.** No staffed booth at the driveway or anywhere on the
  frontage. The only roadside structure is the building's own SE office entry.
- **Remote GS: FALSE.** No gate exists, so no remote-kiosk check-in applies.
- **Dock doors: 50+.** Long continuous dock bank on the full south face (~40+
  doors) PLUS a second full bank on the north face (~30+ doors) = cross-dock,
  comfortably 50+. Overhead estimate.
- **Ship/Rcv separate: TRUE.** Two dock banks on physically distinct building
  faces (south and north aprons).
