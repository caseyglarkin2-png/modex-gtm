# GM - Bay City Powertrain, Bay City MI — Deep Audit

**Facility type:** Powertrain Components Plant
**Address:** 1001 Woodside Ave, Bay City, MI 48708
**Locked center:** 43.6052, -83.8800
**Confidence:** medium

## Location & confirmation

The roster point and a ROOFTOP geocode of 1001 Woodside Ave both resolved to
43.60418, -83.87910 — the south (Woodside Ave) frontage of a large single
manufacturing complex on the east bank of the Saginaw River. I locked the site
center to 43.6052, -83.8800, the centroid of that building.

Web research confirms this is the **active** GM Bay City Powertrain plant:
- GM Authority / DBusiness: ~975,000 sq ft, ~420 UAW Local 362 workers, builds
  camshafts, connecting rods, and block/head machining for GM V8 propulsion.
- Recent investment of $216M+ (part of a larger GM propulsion announcement) to
  retool for future V8 component production — confirms continued operation.
- Plant lineage traces to 1892 (National Cycle Mfg.), into GM in 1918.

Important nuance resolved in Step 0: the cleared land immediately **west and
northwest** of the building (settling/treatment ponds, foundation remnants,
debris fields, dirt service roads — visible at z17-z20) is the **separate ~10-acre
RACER Trust "Bay City Powertrain Industrial Land"** parcel — a former GM
equipment-storage area plus a filled river slip, now vacant redevelopment land.
It is NOT part of the active fenced plant and was excluded from the perimeter.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Site is fully perimeter-fenced (continuous chain-link
  confirmed in Street View). Vehicle/truck access is a controlled curb-cut gate
  from Woodside Ave into the south lot. No barrier-arm detail resolvable at this
  zoom, but the fenced controlled entrance qualifies.
- **Guard shack — FALSE (low confidence).** No distinct staffed booth resolved at
  the Woodside frontage in z20 satellite or 2023 Street View. An older urban plant
  may have a booth just inside the fence not visible from the public road.
- **Remote GS — TRUE (low confidence).** Controlled fenced entrance with no visible
  staffed booth implies kiosk / call-box / badge check-in.
- **Docks — band 10-25.** Truck/material doors are concentrated on the **north
  rail-served face**, not the parking-side south face. Estimated ~18 doors across
  the north face and west service road; old-plant roof clutter and the rail canopy
  obscure an exact count.
- **Drop area — band 0-10; dropYard FALSE.** Very few trailers visible site-wide
  (~4 along the west service road). No dedicated marked trailer-storage lot. This
  is a rail-fed components plant with modest truck dock activity, not a cross-dock DC.

## Final confidence: medium

The facility is positively identified and confirmed active, the perimeter and rail
service are solid, and the urban setting is clear. Confidence is held at medium
because: (1) the guard-shack/remote-check-in call rests on absence of a visible
booth at an older urban plant; (2) truck docks sit on a roof-cluttered, rail-canopied
north face that obscures an exact door count; and (3) trailer/drop counts are low and
hard to pin precisely from overhead.

**3-line summary**
