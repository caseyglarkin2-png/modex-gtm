## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — FALSE.** No barrier arm, sliding/swing gate, or checkpoint
  pinch-point. The driveway from East View Drive is open. Perimeter fencing is
  present around the property but the entrance itself is uncontrolled
  (corroborated by two Street View vintages).
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No small staffed-booth structure beside the driveway.
  The dark peaked-roof structure at the building's south side is the office
  entry portico of the main building, not a guard booth. No booth visible in
  Street View — high confidence.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** No gate, so no remote check-in implied.
- **Docks:** Estimated ~58 total dock doors — east dock line ~30-35, west dock
  line ~20-25. Banded **50+**.
- **Ship/Rcv separate — TRUE.** Two physically separate dock banks on different
  building faces (east face with drop yard; southwest/west face) — consistent
  with separate inbound-receiving and outbound-shipping clusters.
