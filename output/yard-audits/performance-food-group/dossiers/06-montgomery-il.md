## Location confirmation

The roster supplied 41.721277, -88.358237 (ROOFTOP geocode, 1,068 m moved). Satellite
probing at z17-z20 around that point landed squarely on a long NE-SW industrial
building fronting Baseline Rd. Web research confirms 5030 Baseline Rd is the
Performance Foodservice - Chicago broadline DC, the former **Fox River Foods**
headquarters (Fox River Foods was acquired by PFG; the LinkedIn page now reads
"Performance Foodservice - Fox River"). Reported as a ~325,000 sq ft warehouse with
23 refrigerated + 11 dry dock doors, ~100 outbound trucks/day serving 7 states.
Street View signage ("SHIPPING / RECEIVING") on the building wall along Baseline Rd
corroborates the freight identity. Lock is high-confidence.

- **SW end (z18/z20):** The building continues SW; a treeline separates it from the
  neighboring lumber/building-products yard.
