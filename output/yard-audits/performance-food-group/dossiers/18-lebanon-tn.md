## Location resolution
The roster supplied 36.175538, -86.287268 (geocode metadata flagged a 5,129 m
offset from a rough geometric center). Web research confirmed the facility's
true coordinates as ~36.17565, -86.28753 — within ~25 m of the roster pin, so
the roster coordinate was correct; the "offset" was an artifact of the
geocoder's center reference. The facility is Performance Foodservice-Lester,
the PFG Nashville-market broadline DC, an operation tracing back to the 1930
Kenneth O. Lester grocery business and located in the Maddox-Simpson Parkway
industrial park on the north edge of Lebanon, TN (Wilson County). It serves
restaurants/hospitality in TN, southern KY, and northern AL; ~280 employees as
of the 2016 cooler expansion.

Satellite (z16–z19) positively identified the building: a large white-roofed,
L-shaped distribution complex with extensive trailer parking — unambiguous
broadline-DC layout consistent with the facility type.
