## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — FALSE.** No barrier arm, sliding/swing gate, or checkpoint
  pinch-point at the driveway or loop road. Access is an open driveway from the
  public road.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No small staffed-booth structure visible beside the
  driveway or loop road in any satellite view. Street View cannot corroborate
  (no facility-driveway pano), so a small booth cannot be 100 % excluded —
  flagged in `uncertainFields`.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** No gate, so no remote check-in implied.
- **Docks:** Estimated ~42 total dock doors across both buildings (main DC
  ~28-32 on its SW face, secondary building ~10-12). Banded **25-50**; roof
  glare and trailer occlusion make this an estimate.
