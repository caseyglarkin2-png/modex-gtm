# Deep-Audit Dossier — Bulleit Distilling Co. (Shelbyville), KY

**Roster idx:** 6
**Address:** 3464 Benson Pike, Shelbyville, KY 40065
**Facility type:** Distillery
**Locked center:** 38.22280, -85.14320 (production / still-house complex)
**Confidence:** High

## Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (38.22032, -85.145372, geocoded ROOFTOP, moved 25 m)
pinned the **visitor / tasting center** at the south end of the campus, not the
freight-relevant buildings. Satellite probes at z15-z17 revealed the full
campus: a fan of ~10 large two-story rickhouses to the north, a
production/still-house complex in the center, and the visitor center to the
south, all served by a single private drive off Benson Pike. Street View at the
entrance shows a Bulleit Distilling Co. branded tour shuttle, confirming
identity. The freight center of gravity is the production complex, so the locked
center was moved north to 38.22280, -85.14320.

## Key views
- **z15/z16 campus wide:** Whole campus inside woods on a ~300-acre property
  bordered by a reservoir/creek on the west. ~10 large dark rickhouses, central
  production block, visitor center, employee parking, single access drive.
- **z18 still house:** Process complex — fermenters, tanks, columns, silos; a
  paved truck apron on the north/east faces. Tanker loading-rack structures
  visible.
- **z19/z20 grain area:** Production building with process equipment and silos;
  truck apron used for tanker loading/unloading. No conventional dock-door bank.
- **Entrance (Benson Pike T-junction):** Wide landscaped two-lane drive curving
  to a roundabout; lamp posts and decorative planting. No gate.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — FALSE.** The campus access drive off Benson Pike is fully open
  in 2024-11 Street View: no barrier arm, no sliding/swing gate, no checkpoint
  pinch-point. It is a wide landscaped tourist-facing entrance.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed booth near the entrance. The first
  building inside the drive is the public visitor/tasting center.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** No gate, so no remote check-in inferred.
- **Docks — 0-10 (low confidence).** No regular bank of dock-leveler doors.
  Freight is process-style: tanker loading racks at the still house and
  barrel/forklift handling at the rickhouses. ~4 functional truck loading
  positions estimated.
- **Scale — TRUE (medium confidence).** Web research (The Whiskey Wash,
  Distillery Trail) states inbound grain arrives by tractor-trailer and is
  weighed and quality-tested on receipt, so a truck scale exists at the grain
  receiving area. The weigh pad is not crisply resolvable at z20.

## Yard zones and counts
- **Perimeter:** developed operational footprint ~38 acres (full property ~300
  acres, mostly wooded).
- **Dock apron:** one strip on the north/east of the production block where
  tankers and box trucks work.
- **Drop yards / staging:** none distinct — no trailer drop lot observed.
- **Building count:** ~14 (production complex + ~10 rickhouses + visitor center
  + outbuildings).
- **Trailers visible:** ~1; trailer parking capacity ~8 on available apron.
- **Rail served:** no spur into the property.

## Web findings
- $115M facility, opened March 2017, runs 24/7, ~1.8M proof gal/yr (some sources
  cite 6.7M L/yr capacity).
- Inbound grain (15-20M bushels/yr of Shelby County corn) arrives by
  tractor-trailer; grain is weighed and quality-tested on receipt — confirms a
  truck scale.
- Four large modern rickhouses, palletized barrels ~55,000 barrels each.
- 17th member of the Kentucky Bourbon Trail — a public, tour-oriented site.

## Final assessment
Open, tourist-facing distillery campus: no truck gate, no guard shack, long
private approach drive, a grain-receiving truck scale, multi-building campus, no
trailer drop yard, no rail. Confidence **high** on gate/guard-shack/layout;
`dockDoors`, `scale`, and `connectivityIssue` flagged as lower-confidence.
