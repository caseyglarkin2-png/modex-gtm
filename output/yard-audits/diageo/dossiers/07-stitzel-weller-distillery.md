## Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (38.209594, -85.808741, geocoded ROOFTOP, moved 12 m)
landed squarely on the Stitzel-Weller campus. Satellite probes at z16-z17
confirmed the historic distillery footprint: a fan of ~18 multi-story brick
rickhouses, a production/bottling building cluster, and the historic distillery
building now serving as the Bulleit Frontier Whiskey Experience visitor center.
Street View confirmed the brick distillery building and the gated freight
entrance. Locked center placed on the production complex (the freight center of
gravity).

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate — TRUE.** Clear gated freight entrance off Fitzgerald Road:
  a swing/cantilever gate spans the drive with "ENTRANCE" and warning signage.
  The single entrance lane crosses an active rail spur immediately outside.
- **Guard shack — TRUE.** A small light-colored guard-booth structure stands
  beside the entrance lane just inside the gate (a 1-2-vehicle footprint, set
  apart from the rickhouses). Workers in hi-vis vests were present at the gate
  in the captured pano.
- **Remote GS — FALSE.** A staffed guard booth is present, so not remote.
- **Docks — 10-25 (medium confidence).** The production/bottling building has a
  dock bank; z19/z20 imagery shows tankers and trailers backed in. ~14 doors
  estimated.
- **Scale — FALSE (low confidence).** No truck scale resolved in imagery.
- **Drop yard — TRUE.** A long row of ~20+ trailers parks in the central campus
  lot, with additional trailers staged along the Fitzgerald Road frontage.
- **Rail served — TRUE.** An active rail spur runs into the property and past
  the truck gate.

## Web findings
- Historic distillery opened Derby Day 1935; Diageo invested ~$2M to convert it
  into the Bulleit Frontier Whiskey Experience visitor attraction, opened 2015.
- Site continues distilling and maturing on-site (~18 warehouses, ~300K-barrel
  capacity); bottling closed April 2025 with maturation migrating to
  Shelbyville/Lebanon over 2-3 years.
- Public tour site on the Kentucky Bourbon Trail; greeters at the entrance gate
  for visitors — consistent with the staffed gate seen in imagery.

## Final assessment
Gated, guarded distillery/aging campus in urban Shively: confirmed truck gate
with guard booth, rail-served, a real trailer drop yard, multi-building campus,
a production dock bank. Confidence **high** on gate/guard-shack;
`dockDoorCount` and `scale` flagged lower-confidence.
