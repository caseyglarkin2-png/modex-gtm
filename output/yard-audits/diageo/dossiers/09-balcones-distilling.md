## Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (31.550293, -97.135725, geocoded ROOFTOP, moved 19 m)
landed on the Balcones building. Satellite probes at z19-z20 and Street View
confirmed it: a long multi-story historic brick warehouse — the century-old
Texas Fireproof Storage Company building — in downtown Waco, with a distinctive
round rooftop feature. Web research confirms Balcones distills all of its whisky
inside this building. Location confirmed.

## Web findings
- Balcones is a grain-to-glass American single malt distillery in downtown Waco;
  Diageo-owned (acquired 2022).
- All distilling operations are inside the historic Texas Fireproof Storage
  Company building; four copper pot stills built in Scotland.
- Per The Spirits Business (Sept 2025), Diageo halted production at Balcones
  (and George Dickel); the site continues with tasting room / visitor
  operations, so truck activity is minimal.

## Final assessment
Compact downtown urban distillery in a single historic building: no truck gate,
no guard shack, no yard, no drop area, no rail. Loading is street/alley-based
and backup-sensitive. Confidence **high**; only `dockDoorCount` flagged as a
soft estimate.
