## Location confirmation
The roster geocode (41.624158, -88.213886) carried a 4,526 m correction flag.
Web research confirms 24440 W 143rd St is Diageo's legacy Plainfield bottling
plant: opened 1966 as a gin distiller/bottler, expanded with a $120M project in
2013, now Diageo's largest North American bottling operation — five
bottling/packaging lines, ~600 employees, on a 62-acre site (Patch; Diageo
press; D&B). Satellite probing showed the plant occupies the eastern core of
the large connected Diageo Plainfield campus that also includes the Lincolnway
RTD canning facility (roster idx 1) and a process tank farm. Locked center at
41.6245, -88.2140.
