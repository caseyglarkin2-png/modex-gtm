## Location confirmation

Bulleit distillery: a 144-acre property with a 72,000 sq ft distillery and dry

(Diageo press; MarionCountyKY.com; Distillery Trail). Satellite probing around

building with process tanks plus rows of long barrel warehouses on rolling rural
land. Locked center on the distillery building at 37.5453, -85.2790.
