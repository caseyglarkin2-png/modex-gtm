## Web findings
- George Dickel Tennessee whisky; distillery reconstructed 1958 by Schenley's
  Ralph Dupps, ~1 mile from the original site, using Cascade Branch water and
  the Lincoln County Process.
- Ages whisky in single-story sheet-metal warehouses; a small, low-tech,
  hands-on operation.
- Per The Spirits Business, production was paused September 2025; the site
  continues as warehousing/maturation, so freight activity is reduced.
