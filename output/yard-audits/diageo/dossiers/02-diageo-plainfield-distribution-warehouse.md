## Location confirmation
The roster geocode (41.622512, -88.24745) had a 5,222 m correction flag, so it
was not trusted blindly. Web research confirms 25820 W 143rd St is the Diageo
Plainfield Warehouse, operated in conjunction with Midwest Warehouse (the
ImportYeti shipment records list "Diageo Plainfield Warehouse, 25820 W 143rd
Street"; Loc8NearMe / Chamber of Commerce list "Midwest Warehouse" at the same
address). Satellite probes around the geocode point revealed one very large
distribution warehouse on the north side of the W 143rd St corridor, ~2.5 km
west of the Lincolnway canning campus. Locked center at 41.6250, -88.2487.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate: FALSE.** Trucks reach the dock yards from an open E-W access road
  along the south side, through a gap in the employee parking into the east dock
  yard. Street View (2024-07) at the SW and SE corners shows wide open driveways
  with no barrier arm, sliding gate, or checkpoint pinch-point at the public
  road. Only signage posts.
- **Guard shack: FALSE (one ambiguous structure flagged).** No clearly staffed,
  multi-window guard booth at a controlled gate. A small orange structure sits
  at the east dock-yard pinch point (41.6243,-88.2468) among parked trailers; it
  reads as a yard/maintenance office, not a gate booth. There is no barrier arm
  for it to guard. Flagged in uncertainFields.
- **Remote GS: FALSE** — no gate at all.
- **Docks:** True cross-dock — continuous dock doors run nearly the full length
  of BOTH the east and west long faces. Estimated ~130 doors total (band 50+),
  moderate confidence. Ship/receive run from physically separate dock banks on
  opposite faces (shipRcvSeparate true, moderate confidence).

## Web findings

the dossier), operated alongside Midwest Warehouse. Driver reviews mention
appointment-based check-in and multi-hour unload times — consistent with an
appointment-managed dock operation. Open Mon-Fri 7AM-5PM, Sat 24h.
