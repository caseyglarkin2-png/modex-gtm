## Location confirmation
The roster coordinate (17.69168, -64.84829, geocoded GEOMETRIC_CENTER, moved
4 m) landed on a residential coastal area south of Melvin Evans Highway — NOT
the distillery. Web research (PR Newswire, St. Croix Source) places the facility
on property bordering Melvin Evans Highway in Estate Diamond, near Henry E.
Rohlsen Airport. A satellite search along the highway found the large
industrial complex at ~17.7048, -64.8428: a cluster of big white-roofed
distillery/warehouse buildings plus a separate row of long barrel-aging
warehouses to the northwest — consistent with the Captain Morgan rum distillery
and its two ~100K sq ft barrel warehouses. The locked center was moved ~1.6 km
NNE of the roster point.
