## Location confirmation

The roster coordinate (32.089841, -81.134398) landed in the correct light-industrial
pocket off Lissner Avenue near I-516, ~12 minutes from the Port of Savannah. The
exact SalSon building was confirmed against a Colliers / LoopNet commercial
listing for 1501 Lissner Ave, which describes the property explicitly as a
**"Truck Terminal / Container Yard with Crossdock Warehouse"** — 3.75 acres, a
7,800 SF crossdock warehouse, **28 dock doors (expandable to 34)**, and 2,550 SF
of office. salson.com's Savannah page corroborates: "30 cross docking doors as
well as a large container yard," 100% asset-based drayage/transload/cross-dock.

Street View along Lissner Ave shows SalSon-branded tractors and Ryder trailers
staged on the yard. Note: the prominent red-brick two-story office immediately
to the north (visible in 2025 Street View) carries **TForce** signage — that is
a separate neighboring property, not SalSon. The SalSon facility is the long,
low white crossdock warehouse with containers backed in along its dock face.
