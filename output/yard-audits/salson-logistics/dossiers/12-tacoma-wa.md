## Location confirmation

The roster carried no address for Tacoma. Research resolved it: SalSon's Tacoma
port-drayage market is run by **TriPak Inc** — one of the seven companies merged
into SalSon in August 2024 (the dossier's "TriPack Logistics"). TriPak had
earlier been a division of TTSI. A loadmatch directory entry is titled
explicitly **"SalSon Logistics (Tri Pak, Inc. div. of TTSI) / Tacoma, WA."**

TriPak's main Tacoma facility is **2330 Port of Tacoma Rd, Tacoma WA 98421**,
confirmed via Commtrex, Yellow Pages, and a LoopNet/Showcase commercial listing.
The listing describes a ~94,590 SF transload warehouse (built 1966), rail-served,
with roughly 3 acres of paved/fenced yard. TriPak runs 6 facilities around the
Ports of Tacoma and Seattle (~300,000 SF total inside storage, 25 acres outside);
2330 Port of Tacoma Rd is the main one and the address used here.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations

- **Truck gate — FALSE (flagged uncertain).** No barrier arm, sliding gate, or
  checkpoint structure is visible across the truck approach. The warehouse
  fronts a public port-industrial street and trucks pull directly onto the yard
  apron. Some lease copy calls the yard "fenced," but no controlled gate
  structure was imaged — left false, flagged uncertain.
- **Guard shack — FALSE.** No staffed booth at the entry.
- **Remote guard station — FALSE.** No gate, so this is false by definition.
- **Dock doors — "10-25".** Street View shows a row of dock doors with
  intermodal containers backed in on the west face; overhead imagery supports
  the 10-25 band — estimate ~22.
- **Drop area / drop yard — "25-50" / TRUE.** Open paved yard holds drayage
  chassis, tractors and parked containers/trailers.
- **Rail served — TRUE.** A rail spur / classification yard runs immediately
  along the NE side of the warehouse; TriPak is a rail transload specialist and
  advertises 80+ rail car spots (bulk grain, aggregates, food-grade, lumber,
  steel).
