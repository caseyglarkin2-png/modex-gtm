## Location confirmation

The roster coordinate (40.178598, -79.745796) and the address 159 Painter Rd
both resolve to the **Dick's Sporting Goods regional distribution center** in
Smithton, southwestern PA (Westmoreland County). Web research confirms 159
Painter Rd is the Dick's DC complex — Dick's separately lists the DC as 200
Painter Rd, but these are the same fenced campus. Business directories (D&B,
MerchantCircle) place **SalSon Logistics Inc** at 159 Painter Rd as a
truck-transport business — SalSon operates a **dedicated-fleet trucking
terminal serving the Dick's DC** (SalSon's dedicated-fleet line runs DSD/contract
carriage out of retailer DCs). The yard audited here is the Dick's DC site that
SalSon's fleet stages and turns at.
