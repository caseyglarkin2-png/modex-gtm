## Site overview

additions, roughly 600 x 500 ft) in the Gateway business park on the
southeast edge of Beloit, immediately west of Interstate 90. Employee parking
fills the north and northeast (large lots). The truck yard wraps the west and
south sides of the building and is enclosed by chain-link fence with green
privacy slats. A small detached storage shed sits north of the parking lot.
