# Deep-Audit Dossier — Hormel Stockton Plant / Fresno (idx 20)

**Account:** Hormel Foods · **Facility type:** Production Facility
**City:** Fresno, CA · **Roster address:** 3438 W Pontiac Way, Fresno, CA 93722

## Location resolution
The roster address "3438 W Pontiac Way" geocoded into a **residential
neighborhood** in NW Fresno (houses and baseball fields) — clearly wrong. Web
research established the Hormel **Fresno Plant** is the **Corn Nuts** roasting
and packaging facility at **4343 E Florence Ave, Fresno, CA 93725**, in the
southeast Fresno industrial district (Hormel "Our Locations" Fresno Plant +
multiple articles on the Corn Nuts solar array). Satellite probing found a
matching processing plant — solar field, refrigerated/dry trailer docks, a
fenced yard — at **36.7184, -119.7527**. Locked center there. The roster name
also references "Stockton CA"; the audited and confirmed site is the **Fresno**
plant.

## Site overview
A single Corn Nuts processing/packaging building in a dense SE Fresno
industrial area. The whole property is enclosed by a tubular-metal perimeter
fence with a landscaped buffer along E Florence Ave. Employee parking sits
south of the building; the truck yard with loading docks is on the west face.
A large photovoltaic solar array occupies the field north/northeast of the
building (the "Corn Nuts harnesses the power of the sun" installation). A rail
line runs along the east property edge.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate: TRUE (medium confidence).** The property is fully fenced with a
  tubular-metal perimeter fence; vehicle access is via gated driveway openings
  in the fence. Street View only reaches the public road and could not capture
  a gate panel head-on, so the call is medium-confidence and flagged.
- **Guard shack: FALSE.** No staffed booth at any driveway in Street View or
  satellite imagery.
- **Remote GS: TRUE.** Gate present, no guard shack — remote/kiosk check-in
  inferred.
- **Docks: 0-10 band.** ~6-8 dock doors along the west building face with
  green/white trailers backed in; ~10 total estimated.
