# Deep-Audit Dossier — Swiss American Sausage Co. (idx 19)

**Account:** Hormel Foods · **Facility type:** Meat Processing Plant
**City:** Lathrop, CA · **Roster address:** 16500 Harlan Rd, Lathrop, CA 95330

## Location resolution
The roster address "16500 Harlan Rd" geocoded to a **Shell gas station** — an
imprecise/incorrect address. Web research found the actual Swiss American
Sausage Co. address: **251 D'Arcy Parkway, Lathrop, CA 95330**, in the
Crossroads Commerce Center. Satellite probing along D'Arcy Parkway located the
matching facility — a single large meat/sausage processing building with
refrigerated docks, a fenced trailer yard, and a guard-booth entrance — at
**37.8018, -121.2965**. Swiss American (a Hormel/Burke supplier of dry sausage,
pepperoni and pizza toppings, ~110 employees) is confirmed by Hormel "Our
Locations" and press releases about a 2,000-panel solar array at the site.

## Site overview
A single large processing + office building in the planned Crossroads Commerce
Center industrial park in Lathrop (San Joaquin Valley / greater Stockton
metro). Employee/visitor parking sits on the west and north. The truck yard
wraps the south and east of the building, enclosed by perimeter fencing with a
landscaped strip of palm trees along the south edge. The main entrance is a
red-paved apron off D'Arcy Parkway on the west.
