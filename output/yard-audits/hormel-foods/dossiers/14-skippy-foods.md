# Deep-Audit Dossier — Skippy Foods, Little Rock AR (idx 14)

**Account:** Hormel Foods
**Facility type:** Production Facility (Skippy peanut butter — sole global producer)
**Resolved coordinates:** 34.70790, -92.18430
**Confidence:** High

## Location confirmation
The roster address (5111 Fairway Ave, Little Rock) and its geocode landed in a
residential neighborhood — wrong. Web research (Hormel "Our Locations", Little
Rock Chamber, ADEQ permit records) established the Skippy plant's true address
as **8201 Frazier Pike, Little Rock AR 72206**, at the Port of Little Rock, with
coordinates near 34.7072, -92.1850.

A satellite probe there showed a large industrial plant with docks and trailer
yards. 2026 Street View confirmed the **"Skippy" logo on the building tower** —
positive identification. Locked center at 34.70790, -92.18430. The plant has
made Skippy peanut butter since 1977 (166,500 sq ft, ~160 employees, 11
varieties, >3.5M lbs/week); it suffered a fire in September 2025 and returned to
operation by December 2025.
