## Location resolution

The roster supplied only "Chattanooga, TN 37406" with an APPROXIMATE geocode
(35.067592, -85.261899). Step 0 satellite probing showed that point landed on
the **CSX DeButts rail classification yard** — a sea of rail tracks, not a
warehouse. The roster itself notes "exact street address not publicly
confirmed."

Web research resolved Kenco's Chattanooga warehousing:
- Kenco Group HQ is 2001 Riverside Drive, Chattanooga, TN 37406 (an office).
- **Kenco Logistic Services operates warehouses on Enterprise Park Drive**
  (zip 37416, the Enterprise Park / Enterprise South industrial corridor).
  6170 Enterprise Park Dr is a confirmed Kenco building (ROOFTOP geocode
  35.0696387, -85.1823609; Yelp/CoStar "Kenco Logistics Services-Stryker").
- A Kenco affiliate sold a **7-warehouse Chattanooga portfolio for $67.1M in
  2022** (CoStar / Times Free Press), one cited as a 210,000 SF building near
  Enterprise South — matching the BP2 size in this batch and confirming the
  Kenco MCS Chattanooga buildings sit in this industrial cluster.

CH29 / CH7B / BP2 are **internal Kenco building codes**; no public source maps
a code to a specific street address. CH29 (284,720 SF, bulk) was matched to
the largest single-tenant warehouse in the immediate Enterprise Park Dr
cluster — the ~200 m x 130 m white-roof building at 35.0716, -85.1782. This is
a best-fit identification, hence medium confidence.
