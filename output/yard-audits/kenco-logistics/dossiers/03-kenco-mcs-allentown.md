## Location confirmation
The roster pin (40.598247, -75.616265) lands on a dark-roofed warehouse in a
suburban industrial park (Iron Run / Trexlertown area, Lehigh County). Web
research (BBB, Chamber of Commerce, LogiCore) confirms Kenco Logistics
operates at 6975 Ambassador Drive/Rd, Allentown PA 18106. **Decisive
confirmation:** 2025-05 Street View shows the address number "6975" on the
building's SE wall facing Ambassador Drive. Locked center at the building
centroid, ~40.59820 / -75.61650.

**Size discrepancy noted:** the roster source cites 601,836 SF and 92 dock
doors for the Kenco Allentown MCS site. The physical building at the
ROOFTOP-geocoded address is a mid-size warehouse of roughly 150,000-250,000 SF
with a single, modest dock bank — far smaller than 92 doors. The roster figure
most likely aggregates Kenco's total Allentown space (multiple suites or
buildings) or is mislabeled. This audit reports the physical building at the
confirmed address; confidence is Medium because of this mismatch.
