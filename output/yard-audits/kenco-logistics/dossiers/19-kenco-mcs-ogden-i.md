## Location resolution

The roster supplied a ROOFTOP geocode (41.247999, -111.995675) with the
address 1025 Depot Drive, Ogden UT 84404. Step 0 confirmed the point lands
directly on a large distribution warehouse in the **Business Depot Ogden
(BDO)** industrial park.

Web research confirmed 1025 Depot Dr is the **Kenco Oatly Warehouse** — a
finished-goods DC that Kenco built, staffs, and operates for Oatly (the
Swedish oat-milk maker), serving Oatly's Ogden production plant (listed as
1025 Depot Dr, Suite 5). This is a positive identification.
