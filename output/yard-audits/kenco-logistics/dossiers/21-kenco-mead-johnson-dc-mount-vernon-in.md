## CRITICAL SITE STATUS — FACILITY DESTROYED
This is the Mead Johnson Nutrition / Kenco dedicated distribution center for
infant formula. **An EF-3 tornado struck the warehouse on 9 July 2024**,
collapsing the roof and multiple walls and rendering it inoperable (confirmed
by Reckitt press releases, dairyreporter.com, WNIN, and 14News coverage).

Current 2026 Maxar satellite imagery and a 2025-09 Street View pano show the
warehouse has been **fully demolished to a bare concrete slab** — exposed
foundation footings, scattered demolition debris, and earthwork are visible
where a large rectangular distribution building once stood. The 2023-07 Street
View (pre-tornado) shows mature trees screening the road; the 2025-09 pano
shows only the open paved slab behind the tree line. The site is non-operational.
