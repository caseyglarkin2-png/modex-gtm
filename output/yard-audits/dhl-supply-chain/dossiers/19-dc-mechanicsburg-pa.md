## Step 0 — Location resolution

The roster entry had no address and no coordinates ("COULD NOT PIN DOWN"),
noting a leased multi-building DHL presence in the Mechanicsburg/Carlisle
corridor. Web research identified DHL Supply Chain's operating entity in
Mechanicsburg as **Exel Inc. dba DHL Supply Chain (USA)** at addresses on
Salem Church Rd (245 / 260 / 300 / 350). The primary, most-cited operating
DC is **260 Salem Church Rd, Mechanicsburg PA 17050** — a 174,921 sq ft
distribution building on 11.32 acres (built ~1985), owned by LBA Logistics
(sold for a 358% gain in Sept 2024).

The Google Geocoding API returned a ROOFTOP coordinate (40.237151,
-76.998518). A satellite probe there showed a single rectangular
distribution building in a West Shore industrial district. Street View of
the south entrance shows a **DHL Supply Chain monument sign** (yellow/red
DHL logo), positively confirming the building.
