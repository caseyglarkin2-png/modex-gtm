## Step 0 — Location resolution

The roster entry had no address and no coordinates ("COULD NOT PIN DOWN" —
two new facilities, ~450k + ~350k sq ft, completion projected late
2025/2026). Web research established that DHL Supply Chain (Exel Inc.) is
building a two-building, ~800,000 sq ft logistics complex on a ~50-acre
tract in the **Wesley D. Adams Innovation Park** within Apex Industrial
Park, City of North Las Vegas (~$180M investment, 494+ projected jobs;
Nevada GOED tax abatement approved March 2024). No street address has been
published.

Geocoding "Wesley D Adams Ave / Wesley Adams Pkwy" anchored the Apex
sub-area; satellite probing of Apex Industrial Park found a matching
two-building distribution complex at ~36.3454, -114.9345 — two large white-
roofed buildings on a shared truck court, structurally complete. June 2024
user-contributed interior Street View photos show finished, empty warehouse
shells, consistent with a facility in late construction / commissioning.

The two buildings match the published DHL project specs exactly (see below),
confirming the identification.
