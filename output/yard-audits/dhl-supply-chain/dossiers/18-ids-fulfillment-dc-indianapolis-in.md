## Step 0 — Location resolution

The roster entry had no address and no coordinates ("COULD NOT PIN DOWN"),
noting only that it is separate from the Plainfield IN HQ (idx 2). Web
research resolved it: IDS Fulfillment opened its Indianapolis fulfillment
center in 2018 at **5425 Exploration Drive, Indianapolis IN 46241**, in the
Ameriplex business park — the building "World Connect @ Ameriplex -
Building 3", a ~620,935-661,324 sq ft LEED-certified distribution facility
on ~47 acres, across I-70 from Indianapolis International Airport. This is
distinct from the IDS HQ DC in Plainfield (9431 Allpoints Parkway). IDS is
now part of DHL Supply Chain after the May 2025 acquisition.

Satellite probe at the resolved coordinate showed a single very large
rectangular distribution building, confirming the identification.
