# DHL Supply Chain — Facility Verification Rejections

FOV scrub run 2026-06-18 (agent). DHL Supply Chain is a contract-logistics 3PL:
it operates DCs FOR clients, almost always in leased buildings, and these
contracts churn hard. Of 24 candidate sites: **11 confirmed, 4 probable
(caveated), 9 rejected.**

## Probable — ship caveated and capped (4)

- **Groveport DC (6390 Commerce Ct, Groveport OH)** — PROBABLE. Only directory/
  state-registry data place DHL (Exel Inc dba DHL Supply Chain) at 6390 Commerce
  Ct; no dated Tier-1 press. The Groveport/Rickenbacker submarket is churning
  (DHL closed nearby 5235 Westpoint Dr late-2023, 264 jobs), but no negative
  against 6390 Commerce specifically. Confirm operator before relying on it.

- **Keurig DC (6000 Studio Way, Union City GA)** — PROBABLE. The DHL/Keurig tie at
  6000 Studio Way rests only on crowdsource data (Waze/Nextdoor/Foursquare); no
  Tier-1. CRITICAL distinction: the headline Feb-2025 DHL Union City closure is on
  Derrick Industrial Parkway (idx 13), a DIFFERENT building, so it does not
  condemn Studio Way. Active Union City DHL hiring supports continued operation.
  Verify the Keurig contract is still live before treating this as a Keurig
  account.

- **DHL Commerce Park DC (Winding Woods Road, St. George SC)** — PROBABLE. DHL
  owns DHL Commerce Park (bought 125 acres; 3 buildings / 1.7M sq ft / $100M) and
  it was built/operating by 2021. No closure/contract-loss found, but no clean
  post-2022 Tier-1 confirming current operation. Tenancy = owned. Confirm with
  FOV imagery (buildings built + DHL-branded).

- **DC — Edgerton KS / Logistics Park Kansas City** — PROBABLE. DHL Supply Chain
  has a confirmed current presence in Edgerton/LPKC (active hiring) but no Tier-1
  enumerates the exact street address; a directory pins 32190 W 191st St,
  Edgerton, KS 66030. Flag the address before audit.
