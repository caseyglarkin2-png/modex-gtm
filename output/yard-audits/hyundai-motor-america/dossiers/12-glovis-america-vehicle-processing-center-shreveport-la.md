## Step 0 — Location confirmation
The roster geocode flagged a 10,038 m move, so location was verified carefully.
A z16 satellite probe at the roster point lands directly on the former General
Motors Shreveport Operations assembly plant — a sprawling industrial building
complex. Web research (KTBS, KSLA, Louisiana Economic Development,
opportunitylouisiana.gov) confirms Glovis America announced a vehicle processing
and distribution hub at the former GM plant in Caddo Parish, occupying a
125,000-sqft facility plus ~90 adjoining acres of the much larger former-GM
campus. Glovis outfits imported vehicles with dealer-requested equipment. This is
a positive ID; center kept on the main building at 32.426875, -93.920747.

## Key views
- **z14 context** — the former GM plant sits in a semi-rural / light-industrial
  zone on the edge of Shreveport, surrounded by forest and cleared land.
- **z17 plant core** — a massive interconnected former auto-assembly building
  complex; the roster point is on the building. Much of the complex appears
  partly disused.
- **z18 vstore probe (32.4335,-93.923)** — an active vehicle-storage marshaling
  lot holding several hundred finished vehicles in marked rows, with rail spurs
  alongside.
- **z18 east / railyard probes** — a substantial rail facility on the east side;
  Street View (2024-10) confirms auto-rack and boxcar railcars parked on multiple
  tracks.
- **Street View** — perimeter roads (2021-02, 2024-10) are wide divided roads but
  empty, weed-grown and decaying, consistent with a largely vacant former plant.
  No controlled, staffed truck gate was visible from public road imagery.

## Gate / guard-shack / dock determinations
- **Truck gate:** TRUE (medium-low confidence). Glovis runs a secured vehicle-
  processing operation, so a controlled entrance is assumed. However, the
  perimeter access roads visible in Street View are open and disused; a manned
  checkpoint could not be positively confirmed and the gate area could not be
  boxed.
- **Guard shack:** FALSE / **remoteGs:** TRUE (best estimate, flagged uncertain).
  No staffed booth identified in available imagery.
- **Dock doors:** 10-25 band (flagged uncertain). The legacy GM plant has truck
  docks on its building faces; the active count is uncertain given partial
  disuse. Vehicle movement is mainly by car-carrier and rail.

## Yard zones and counts

- **Drop yard:** active vehicle-marshaling lot in the NW portion (several hundred

- **Buildings:** ~8 distinct structures across the interconnected plant complex

- **Rail-served:** TRUE — active east-side rail yard with auto-racks and spurs

- **Truck gate / staging:** could not be reliably boxed; left null.

## Web findings

  and distribution hub at the former GM plant in Caddo Parish, creating 150 jobs;
  125,000-sqft facility on 90+ adjoining acres.
- KSLA: earlier Hyundai Glovis announcement of 396 jobs at the former GM plant.

## Final confidence
**Medium.** Facility identity, type, rail service, the active vehicle-marshaling
lot, the multi-building former-plant campus, and the rural setting are clearly
established. Gate control, guard-shack presence, lane counts, the exact active
dock count, and post-gate staging could not be positively confirmed — the
perimeter is open and partly disused with no interior Street View — and are
listed as uncertain.
