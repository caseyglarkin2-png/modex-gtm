## Location confirmation (Step 0)

The supplied geocode (41.921573, -84.615533, GEOMETRIC_CENTER) landed on the
plant complex. Satellite at z16-z20 shows a multi-building food-processing
campus set well back from public roads, surrounded by residential streets,
farm fields, and woodland on the edge of Hillsdale. Street View (captured
2024-09) from the SW access road shows a "Bob Evans Farms" entrance sign and
the plant building branded with Bob Evans red script — positive ID. Note: the
address "200 N Wolcott St" is the postal address, but Wolcott St itself is
residential and the plant is set back behind houses; the actual truck/vehicle
access is a long private drive that connects to a side street SW of the site.
Web research confirms this is the company's third plant, producing fresh pork
sausage since 1964, 50+ SKUs, 25M+ lb/yr; BEF Foods operates it as a sow
slaughter / sausage facility.
