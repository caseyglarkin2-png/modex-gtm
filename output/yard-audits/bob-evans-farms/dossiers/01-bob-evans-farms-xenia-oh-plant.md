on the plant complex. Satellite probes at z16-z20 show a multi-section
industrial / food-processing building with rooftop process equipment, an

Street View (captured 2022-06) along Birch Rd shows the building branded
"Bob Evans" in the company's signature red script on both the west office wing
(with red awning and US flag) and the main plant wall — positive ID. Web
research corroborates: 640 Birch Rd is the historic Xenia sausage plant, in
operation since 1953, ~30,000 sq ft core, >100 employees, ~30M lb/yr fresh
product; subject of a $6.15M expansion announced April 2014. This is the
plant, not a restaurant. Note: a Yelp listing tags this address as "CLOSED" —
likely a listing-data artifact for the consumer-facing entry; the facility is
the active manufacturing plant per the company's About Us page.
