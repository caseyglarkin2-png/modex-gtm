## Location confirmation (Step 0)

The supplied geocode (43.684086, -111.900523, ROOFTOP) landed on the plant
complex. Satellite at z16-z20 shows a large multi-building potato-processing
plant along N Yellowstone Hwy, surrounded by farm fields with a highway
interchange nearby. Web research confirms 398 N Yellowstone Hwy is the Potato
Products of Idaho (PPI) plant — a frozen/dehydrated potato-products
manufacturer established 1998, acquired by Post Holdings (parent of the Bob
Evans Refrigerated Retail business). The roster note matches: Post folded PPI
into the Refrigerated Retail network. Street View from the frontage road
showed an adjacent "BV's PRODUCE" sign (Rigby Produce, a neighboring business)
— the PPI plant itself is the large building cluster, positively located.
