# 08 — Gatorade, Oakland CA (1175 57th Ave, Oakland 94621)

## Location confirmation
Rooftop geocode landed on a white-roofed plant block fronting 57th Ave in East
Oakland. Street View (2025-05) confirms: a 1929-vintage brick industrial front
(matches the "built 1929, former pork-and-bean cannery" history; PepsiCo
acquired it in the 2001 Quaker merger, ~157k sq ft) with a Gatorade-liveried
trailer backed into a street-side dock, plus Cheema Freight Lines and tanker
trailers on the curb. Confirmed — this is the Gatorade bottling plant.

  third-party warehouse (NW), San Leandro St-side street (NE/SE), and 57th Ave

  the lined trailer stalls beyond the drive belong to the neighbor.
- SV pano 5DsSXmn5cvdHeQX_mX1ORA (57th Ave at the SW drive): open drive into
