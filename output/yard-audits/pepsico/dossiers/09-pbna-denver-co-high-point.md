# 09 — PBNA Denver CO, High Point (19300 E 72nd Ave / 6972 N Argonne St)

## Location confirmation
Rooftop geocode lands on the eastern half of a ~140-acre graded parcel south of
the E 72nd Ave corridor near DIA, holding one enormous (~1.2M sq ft) blue/white
roof building — unambiguously the new PBNA High Point mega-plant (152-acre land
buy per PR Newswire; largest PBNA US plant; active per Denverite, Mar 2025).

## The catch: imagery predates completion
- Maxar satellite (all zooms) shows MID-CONSTRUCTION: exposed steel framing on
  the east production hall, material laydown yards, construction trailers.
- Nearest Street View is 2021-08 — a barricaded road stub in bare prairie,
  pre-construction. No post-opening ground imagery exists yet.
So the finished truck gate, guard shack, dock count, and trailer yard cannot be
observed. Everything beyond the perimeter is flagged.

## What was determined
- **Perimeter (solid):** grading limits are crisp — E 72nd Ave corridor (N),
  cultivated field line (E), diagonal drainage canal (SE), roundabout access
  road (S), creek-corridor pad edge (W). 8-vertex ring, 140.6 acres by
  shoelace vs 152 reported — consistent (corner cuts explain the gap).
- **Gate/guard:** NOT observable; left false and flagged. A new-build flagship
  of this scale is almost certainly gated — re-audit when fresh imagery lands.
- **Docks/drop:** 50+ both, inferred from the 1.2M sq ft footprint and graded
  yard pads, flagged uncertain. Single access from the S roundabout (Argonne).
- **Setting:** Urban (Denver metro edge, subdivisions immediately west).
- **Rail:** none into the parcel.

## Web findings

## Confidence
