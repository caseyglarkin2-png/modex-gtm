# 07 — Gatorade, Mountain Top PA (750 Oak Hill Rd, Crestwood Industrial Park)

## Location confirmation
Roster rooftop geocode (41.129431, -75.886141) landed dead-center on a ~24-acre
single-tenant plant: one large connected manufacturing/warehouse complex with
trailer rows on three sides, in a wooded industrial park with a rail corridor on
the south. Street View frames show Pepsi-branded trailers (plus Swift, Western
Express, XTRA, Schneider, Bowman) parked along the west lot — consistent with
the PepsiCo/Gatorade plant in Crestwood Industrial Park. Confirmed.

## Determinations
- **Gate: NONE** — open driveways, SV traversal proves no control point.
- **Guard shack: NONE** — no booth at any entrance.
- **Docks:** west bank ~10-12 doors (trailers backed in), notch bank ~8-10,
  more positions on the east face → band **25-50** (est. ~30).
- **Drop yard: YES** — three traced trailer lots (W ~20 stalls, NE ~12, E ~25)
  plus overflow; ~75 trailers visible, capacity ~110 → dropArea **50+**.
- **Setting:** Rural (small-town wooded industrial park, Mountain Top PA), but
  on the I-81 corridor near Wilkes-Barre → no connectivity concern.
- **Rail:** line along south boundary, no spur into the parcel.

## Zones traced

(~24.3 acres by shoelace). Truck gate zone = NW driveway throat. 3 drop yards,
2 dock aprons (west bank + notch bank).
