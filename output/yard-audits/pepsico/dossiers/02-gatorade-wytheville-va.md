# 02 — Gatorade (PepsiCo Blue Ridge) — Wytheville, VA

**Resolved location:** 316 Gator Ln, Wytheville VA 24382 (Progress Park, I-77/I-81) — campus centered at **36.9663, -81.0145**. Roster rooftop geocode landed on the plant; confirmed.
**Maps:** https://www.google.com/maps/@36.9663,-81.0145,400m/data=!3m1!1e3

## Identity confirmation (Step 0)
- z16/z17 satellite shows a ~700 m long production building (long axis WSW-ENE, rotated ~17 degrees off E-W) plus a second large finished-goods warehouse to the SE, connected by an elevated conveyor bridge — exactly the Gatorade greenfield campus the Wythe County JIDA announced. Isolated greenfield site surrounded by pasture; the only access is the Gator Ln drive. The separate 200 Pepsi Way bottling site is NOT in this perimeter.

## Determinations
- **Gate:** `truckGate: true` — controlled checkpoint ~160 m inside the property entrance.
- **Guard shack:** `true` — booth-scale structure beside the lane with a truck stopped at it (satellite + SV agreement). `remoteGs: false`.
- **Docks:** band **50+** — ~50 doors along the plant's south face plus ~25 on the warehouse west face (est. 75 total).
- **Drop yard:** `true`, band **50+** — a second full-length trailer row (~60-80 trailers) parallel to and south of the dock apron.
- **Layout:** one entrance (entry/exit together, 1 lane each side of the booth + auto lane), long driveway, pre-gate shoulder staging, wide apron = fast-lane opportunity. Ship/receive separate (plant docks vs warehouse docks). Campus = `multipleFacilities: true`. Rural (edge-of-town greenfield) but on the I-77/I-81 interchange, so no connectivity concern. No rail spur, no visible scale (flagged uncertain).
