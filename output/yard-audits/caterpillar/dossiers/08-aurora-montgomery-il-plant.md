# Deep-Audit Dossier — Caterpillar Aurora (Montgomery) IL Plant

## Location confirmation
Roster geocode (41.714988, -88.363515, ROOFTOP, moved 2745 m) lands inside a very
large industrial campus. Web search confirmed this is the former Caterpillar
Aurora/Montgomery large-wheel-loader plant — a ~350-acre, 4M+ sq ft campus that
shut production in 2017 and is now marketed for lease/sale as "The Grid at
Route 31" (Reich Brothers / LoopNet listings; Equipment World reporting). The
locked center sits in the middle of the main manufacturing complex.

## Key views
- **Wide (z15):** Sprawling multi-building manufacturing campus with a rail
  corridor along the east edge, large open material yards to the south, and an
  open-land buffer to the west toward Route 31.
- **NW main entrance (z19/z20):** A divided entry boulevard runs off Route 31 to
  the buildings; a substantial pyramid-roof gatehouse sits in the median between
  the inbound and outbound lanes.
- **Street View (2018, heading 180 into the entrance):** Clear view straight up
  the divided drive — gatehouse visible in the median, CATERPILLAR sign on the
  main building to the left, perimeter chain-link fencing along the road.
- **North building (z19):** Trailers backed into the south face plus a cluster
  of ~8-10 parked trailers and additional trailers to the east.
- **South campus (z16/z18):** Large outdoor storage yards full of rows of
  material/equipment, and a rail spur entering the property.

## Web findings
Caterpillar wound down production here in 2017 (large wheel loaders moved to
Decatur IL, medium loaders to North Little Rock AR), cutting ~800 jobs. The
site is now "The Grid at Route 31," marketed for lease/sale by Reich Brothers.
Audited as the physical industrial campus regardless of current ownership.
