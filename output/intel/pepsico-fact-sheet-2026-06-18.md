  states the goal is **"10 to 15 percent reductions in capital expenditure by
  uncovering hidden capacity,"** with **"a 20 percent increase in throughput on

    capacity"). Lead the why-now with it.

  figure is secondary/estimated — do not state as fact.)

  CONFIRMED): *"Serving our vast network of customers requires a supply chain that is

  consistently for our customers."*

## Multi-format complexity [PUBLIC]
- Yard complexity = **largest private DSD network in NA interleaving with bulk
  inbound/outbound truckload**, across dry snacks (Frito-Lay) and beverages
  (PBNA, company + bottler). Quaker is warehouse-delivered shelf-stable. Chilled is
  a minority stream.

## Detention — use the INDUSTRY benchmark, never a fake PepsiCo number
