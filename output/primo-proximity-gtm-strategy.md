## 1. Executive Summary

### The play

Primo Brands (NYSE: PRMB, $5.15B revenue, 13,000 employees) is FreightRoll's marquee customer. They are running FreightRoll solutions -- Driver Journey, YMS, RTLS, and Machine Vision Gate -- at 28 sites today and are ripping out PINC to roll FreightRoll out to all 260 of their facilities. The "Primo Proximity" campaign uses this anchor customer as social proof to win their literal neighbors: companies with truck yards within 50 miles of a live Primo site.

### Why it works

1. **FOMO.** "Your neighbor is already running this" is the most visceral proof point in enterprise sales. It is not a case study on a PDF -- it is a live operation happening down the road.
2. **CTO convenience.** FreightRoll's CTO is already traveling to Primo sites for installs and check-ins. Swinging by a prospect's yard next door with cameras to scope a pilot is a genuine, low-friction offer -- not a gimmick.
3. **Same logistics corridor.** Companies clustered in the same industrial parks face the same driver pool, the same carrier base, and the same yard congestion. What works at Primo works at the neighbor.

### Boston Beer proof point

This is not theory. Boston Beer Company's CSCO Phil Savastano replied to a cold proximity email within 1 hour. Their Samuel Adams Pennsylvania Brewery in Breinigsville, PA is 2.5 miles from Primo's Allentown factory. The "neighbor" hook converted a cold outreach into a warm conversation in under 60 minutes.

### Scale

- **41 of 43** audited prospect accounts have at least one facility within 50 miles of a Primo site
- **175 total proximity matches** across those 41 accounts
- **29 Tier 1 accounts** with multiple matches and/or sub-10-mile proximity
- **5 Tier 2 accounts** with single close matches or moderate proximity
- **7 Tier 3 accounts** with single matches at 40-50 miles

## 3. Messaging Framework

### The "neighbor" hook formula

Every outreach follows this structure:

```
1. OPEN:    "GM -- small world." + proximity fact (distance, same city/park)
2. BRIDGE:  What FreightRoll is doing at the nearby Primo site (specific solutions)
3. SCALE:   How many of their facilities are near Primo sites
4. OFFER:   CTO onsite visit -- "swing by with a couple cameras and scope a pilot"
5. PROOF:   "Ripping out PINC, rolling to 260 sites, ships more product"
```

### Solutions to highlight based on nearby Primo site

| Primo Solution Running Nearby | What to Highlight | Key Stat |
|-------------------------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Machine Vision Gate** | Camera reads trailer IDs at entry/exit, auto-updates YMS | Check-in drops from 12 min to 90 seconds |
| **YMS (Yard Management System)** | Real-time trailer location, dock assignment, no windshield surveys | "No more radio calls, no more lost trailers" |
| **Driver Journey** | Driver self-check-in on phone, guided to dock, no clipboards | Eliminates guard shack bottleneck |
| **RTLS (Real-Time Location System)** | Continuous trailer tracking across the yard, autonomous dash-cam on spotters | "Know where every trailer is, always" |

### Key proof points (use in every outreach)

1. **"Ripping out PINC, rolling to 260 sites"** -- Primo chose FreightRoll over the incumbent. This is a validated replacement, not a science project.
2. **"Sites with our solutions ship significantly more product"** -- The ROI is measured in throughput, not just efficiency.
3. **Machine vision gate cuts check-in from 12 min to 90 seconds** -- Concrete, memorable stat.
4. **Autonomous yard spotter dash-cam** -- Camera on the spotter tractor reads trailer IDs while driving, updating location in real time. No manual scans.
5. **Camera-based, pilot takes days not months** -- Low infrastructure, fast time-to-value.

### Industry-specific hooks

| Industry | Hook Variation |
|----------|---------------|
| **Beverage** (Niagara, KDP, Coca-Cola, AB InBev, Constellation) | "Same business -- high-volume bottling, massive trailer counts, tons of drop-and-hook. Primo's already proving this works for water. It works for [beer/soda/juice] the same way." |
| **3PL** (CJ Logistics, Kenco, GXO, DHL, SalSon, Universal) | "Your customers would love seeing real-time yard visibility on their freight. This is a differentiator you can sell." |
| **Retail DC** (Home Depot, Walmart, Costco, UNFI, H-E-B) | "At the volume your DCs move, every minute of gate dwell time costs real money. Machine vision at the gate eliminates the bottleneck." |
| **Auto OEM** (Honda, Toyota, Hyundai, Daimler, Ford) | "Parts to dealers on tight timelines. Real-time yard visibility means nothing sits when it shouldn't." |
| **CPG/Food** (Frito-Lay, General Mills, Campbell's, Mondelez, PFG, Hormel) | "High-SKU, high-frequency shipping. Knowing which trailer has what and where it's parked = faster loads, fewer errors." |

## 5. Campaign Metrics & Goals

### Benchmarks (based on Boston Beer proof point)

The Phil Savastano reply (CSCO, <1 hour, cold email) sets an aggressive but real benchmark. Adjust for the fact that not every recipient is a CSCO reading email at 7:30 AM.

| Metric | Conservative | Target | Stretch |
|--------|-------------|--------|---------|
| **Open rate** | 45% | 55% | 65% |
| **Reply rate** | 8% | 12% | 18% |
| **Meeting rate** (from replies) | 40% | 50% | 60% |
| **Meetings booked** (Wave 1, 15 accounts) | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| **Meetings booked** (all 41 accounts) | 5 | 8 | 12 |

### Pipeline targets

| Stage | Volume | Avg Deal Size (est.) | Pipeline Value |
|-------|--------|---------------------|----------------|
| Meetings booked | 8 | -- | -- |
| Discovery / pilot scoped | 5 | $150K ARR | $750K |
| Pilot running | 3 | $150K ARR | $450K |
| Closed-won (12-month horizon) | 2 | $200K ARR (expanded scope) | $400K ARR |

### Success criteria

- **Week 2 checkpoint:** At least 3 replies from Wave 1 sends. If below 3, revise subject lines and resend non-openers with new angle.
- **Week 4 checkpoint:** At least 2 meetings booked across all waves. If below 2, escalate to multi-threaded outreach (hit 2-3 contacts per account simultaneously).
- **Week 8 checkpoint:** At least 1 pilot scoped. If zero, reassess messaging and consider warm intro paths (Primo contacts, shared investors, conference overlap).
