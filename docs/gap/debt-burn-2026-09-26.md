## Routing benchmark

`scripts/gap/bench-routing.ts`, dry run (no row, no audit), same cohort both times: the 7 accounts
in use in production (PepsiCo, Kroger, General Mills, The Home Depot, Coca-Cola, Unfi, FedEx), real
Postgres, real HubSpot reads and the real Clawd suppression read, run from a workstation (about
140ms per DB round trip, about 3.3s per Clawd read). Two runs each.

| cohort | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 1 account (PepsiCo, 9 people) | 45-57s | 17-20s |
| 5 accounts (22 people) | 107-111s | 37-41s |
| 7 accounts (27 people) | 130-135s | 44-48s |
| APPROVE + USE one account (PepsiCo, 4 people in use) | whole routable scope, 7 accounts: 130-135s | targeted: 7-10s |

The per-person Clawd read dominates; after = 3 accounts and 4 people at once, and the Clawd read
overlaps each person's DB reads. Production DB round trips are shorter than a workstation's, so
production times should be at or below these.
